Shading & Soiling Loss Estimator

Estimate the extra production loss from site-specific shading and dirt on the panels
Solar Index SO.01 Sizing Tools SO.04 Shading & Soiling SO.02 Notes
Standard production estimates already bake in an average system-loss allowance, but a specific site can lose meaningfully more to a nearby tree or chimney, or to dust, pollen and bird droppings between cleanings. This gives a rough annual loss percentage for your site's shading and soiling conditions — use it to sanity-check a quote or decide whether panel cleaning is worth scheduling.
1

Shading Loss

How much of the array is affected by nearby obstructions — trees, chimneys, adjacent buildings, roof vents — at any point in the day.
Midday shading costs more than the same shading confined to morning or afternoon, since that's when the array would otherwise produce the most.

How this is calculated
Combined loss = 1 − (1 − shading) × (1 − soiling) — losses compound rather than add, so two 10% losses combine to about 19%, not 20%.
Cleaning frequency scales down the soiling figure: quarterly cleaning roughly halves it, monthly cleaning cuts it to about a quarter of the never-cleaned figure.
These are typical first-pass ranges, not a substitute for a shading analysis (e.g. a solar path/obstruction survey) or manufacturer soiling data for your specific region. Treat this as an extra deduction on top of — not instead of — the standard system-loss allowance already built into most production estimates.
