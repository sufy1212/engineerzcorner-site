Row Spacing & Self-Shading Calculator

Minimum row-to-row gap to keep one row from shading the next
Solar Index SO.01 Sizing Tools SO.13 Row Spacing SO.02 Notes
Rows of tilted panels cast a longer shadow the lower the sun sits, and the sun sits lowest at solar noon on the winter solstice — the standard worst case used for row-spacing rules of thumb. Space rows too tightly and the front of one row shades the row behind it for part of the day, cutting its output.
