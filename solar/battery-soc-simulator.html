How this is calculated
Generation is modelled as a half-sine over a fixed 06:00–18:00 daylight window: P(t) = A·sin(π(t−6)/12), with amplitude A set so the window's total energy equals array power × peak sun hours, capped at the array's rated power.
Load is spread evenly across the day: Pload = daily load ÷ 24.
Each hour, net power charges or discharges the battery: charging energy is derated by round-trip efficiency (ΔWh = Pnet·1h·η), discharging is not (round-trip loss is booked on the way in). SOC is clamped between 0% and 100% of usable capacity.
Minimum SOC is the lowest point reached anywhere in the 24-hour run, compared against your floor to flag undersizing.
Simplified single fixed-shape generation curve and flat load profile for quick first-pass sizing/teaching — not a substitute for hourly irradiance data (PVGIS/NREL) and your actual load schedule.
Rule-of-thumb 24-hour SOC trace for a quick undersizing/floor-margin check — verify against real hourly irradiance and load data before final battery sizing.
