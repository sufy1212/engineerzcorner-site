Full Solar Symbol Reference

Every symbol above, with its full meaning and typical usage in one scannable table — useful for a quick page search or side-by-side comparison instead of clicking through each tile individually.

SymbolCategoryMeaningWhere it's used
PV Module / PanelPV ComponentsA rectangle divided into a grid — represents a single photovoltaic module (panel), the basic building block of a solar array.Single-line diagrams and layout drawings at every module location, individually or grouped into a string.
PV ArrayPV ComponentsA group of module rectangles shown together — represents the complete assembly of modules wired into strings, the DC source for the whole system.Site layout and single-line diagrams to show the array as a single block rather than every individual module.
Solar CellPV ComponentsA small square with a horizontal busbar line — the individual photovoltaic cell, dozens of which are wired together inside one module.Technical datasheets and cross-section drawings illustrating module construction, rather than system-level diagrams.
Junction BoxPV ComponentsA small enclosure mounted on the module back — houses the module's bypass diodes and the output leads/connectors.Module wiring diagrams and installation drawings, at the rear-mounted exit point of a module's DC output.
Bypass DiodePV ComponentsA triangle-and-bar diode symbol — routes current around a shaded or faulted cell group so it doesn't limit the whole module's output or overheat.Module internal wiring diagrams, typically one diode per cell group inside the junction box.
Mounting Rail / RackingPV ComponentsA horizontal rail with support legs — the structural framework that holds modules at the correct tilt and spacing.Structural and installation drawings showing the mounting system a module array is secured to.
String InverterInverters & Power ConversionA box with a stylized waveform — converts the combined DC output of one or more module strings into grid-compatible AC.Single-line diagrams between the DC combiner/array and the AC disconnect, for systems using one central string-level inverter.
MicroinverterInverters & Power ConversionA small inverter icon shown directly beneath a module symbol — converts each individual module's DC output to AC right at the panel.Single-line diagrams for systems where each module has its own inverter rather than one shared string inverter.
Central InverterInverters & Power ConversionA large enclosure icon — a single large-capacity inverter serving an entire commercial or utility-scale array, larger than a typical string inverter.Single-line diagrams for commercial/utility-scale systems consolidating many array sub-fields into one power conversion point.
Charge Controller (MPPT/PWM)Inverters & Power ConversionA box with an angled tracking-curve icon — regulates DC power from the array to safely charge a battery bank, tracking the array's optimal operating point (MPPT) or simple on/off switching (PWM).Off-grid and hybrid system single-line diagrams, between the array and the battery bank.
Combiner BoxInverters & Power ConversionAn enclosure with multiple parallel input lines converging to one output — joins several module strings' outputs into a single combined DC feed, typically with individual string fusing.Single-line diagrams between multiple parallel strings and a shared inverter or charge controller input.
MC4 ConnectorWiring & ConnectionsTwo interlocking circular connector halves — the standard weatherproof, locking DC connector joining module and string cables in the field.Field wiring drawings and installation guides at every module-to-module and module-to-combiner connection point.
DC Disconnect SwitchWiring & ConnectionsA break in the line with an angled blade — a manually operated switch that isolates DC power between the array and the inverter/charge controller.Single-line diagrams at the required DC isolation point ahead of the inverter, for safe servicing.
AC Disconnect SwitchWiring & ConnectionsThe same switch symbol boxed to denote AC service — isolates AC power between the inverter output and the utility connection point.Single-line diagrams at the required AC isolation point, typically adjacent to the utility meter or main panel.
Conduit RunWiring & ConnectionsA pair of parallel lines with end caps — represents a length of electrical conduit protecting cable runs between equipment.Site layout and installation drawings tracing the physical routing of DC and AC cable between the array and equipment.
Grounding ConductorWiring & ConnectionsA line terminating in three descending horizontal bars — the standard electrical ground symbol, marking a conductor bonded to earth.Single-line and grounding/bonding diagrams at every equipment grounding and system grounding connection point.
Battery BankStorageA rectangular cell body with a small terminal cap — represents the battery bank storing DC energy for later use.Single-line diagrams for off-grid and hybrid systems, between the charge controller and the battery disconnect/inverter.
Battery Management System (BMS)StorageA monitoring box shown integrated with the battery bank — protects and balances individual cells/modules, managing charge, discharge, and temperature limits.Battery system diagrams, particularly lithium battery banks where cell-level protection and balancing is essential.
Battery DisconnectStorageA disconnect switch symbol positioned at the battery terminals — allows the battery bank to be isolated from the rest of the system for safe servicing.Single-line diagrams immediately at the battery bank output, required ahead of the inverter/charge controller connection.
Charge/Discharge Current MeterStorageA circular meter face marked A (amps) — displays real-time current flowing into or out of the battery bank.Battery monitoring panels and off-grid system dashboards, tracking state of charge trends alongside voltage.
Utility Meter (Bi-Directional)Metering & MonitoringA meter face with a two-directional arrow — measures energy flowing both from the grid to the site and, under net metering, from the site back to the grid.Single-line diagrams at the utility point of interconnection for grid-tied and hybrid systems.
Production MeterMetering & MonitoringA dedicated meter symbol placed at the inverter's AC output — measures the solar system's total energy production independent of the utility meter.Single-line diagrams for systems needing separate production verification, such as incentive programs or performance monitoring.
Monitoring Data LoggerMetering & MonitoringA box with a small bar-chart icon — collects performance data from the inverter and other devices for remote monitoring and analysis.System architecture diagrams showing how production and status data reaches an owner's or installer's monitoring platform.
Rapid Shutdown DeviceMetering & MonitoringA power-button style icon at module level — a module-level device that drops array conductors to a safe low voltage on command, for firefighter and installer safety.Rooftop single-line diagrams where code requires rapid shutdown capability at or near each module.
DC Fuse / BreakerSafety & ProtectionA rectangular box in the DC line — a protective device that opens the circuit if current exceeds a safe rated level, protecting conductors from overcurrent.Single-line and combiner box diagrams, individually per string, protecting each string conductor.
Surge Protection Device (SPD)Safety & ProtectionA lightning-bolt style icon connected across the line — diverts transient overvoltage (from lightning or switching surges) safely to ground, protecting sensitive equipment.Single-line diagrams at the DC combiner and the AC service point, especially in lightning-prone regions.
Arc-Fault Circuit Interrupter (AFCI)Safety & ProtectionA jagged spark-like line inside a protective device symbol — detects a dangerous arcing condition in DC wiring (often from a loose or damaged connection) and interrupts the circuit.Inverter and combiner single-line diagrams, required by code on many rooftop DC circuits.
Ground-Fault Protection Device (GFDI)Safety & ProtectionA circle with a diagonal fault line — detects unintended current leaking to ground from the DC system and interrupts the circuit to prevent shock or fire hazard.Inverter-integrated protection shown on single-line diagrams, monitoring the DC array for insulation faults.
