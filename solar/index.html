Solar — Calculator Index

Fig. S1 — Sun to panel to battery, one string
Array sizing, battery storage and inverter sizing, chained together on one sheet — plus PV string voltage checks, a payback/ROI calculator, tilt & orientation guidance, a live 24-hour battery state-of-charge simulator, a learning-notes reference, a click-to-explore symbol library, step-by-step engineering guides, and head-to-head equipment comparisons. First-pass sizing for budgeting and layout; always confirm against a manufacturer's design tool and your site's actual irradiance data before ordering equipment.
SO.01
Solar Sizing Tools
Array sizing (kWp and panel count from a daily energy target), battery storage sizing (kWh and Ah from load, autonomy and DoD), and inverter sizing (kVA from a DC:AC ratio) — three chained steps on one sheet.
SO.05
PV String Sizing
Cold-weather string Voc and hot-weather string Vmp from module datasheet values, checked against the inverter's max input voltage and MPPT window.
SO.06
Payback & ROI
Simple payback period and lifetime ROI from system cost, incentives, production, rate escalation, degradation and O&M cost.
SO.07
Tilt & Orientation
Recommended fixed tilt angle and azimuth from site latitude — year-round, winter-weighted or summer-weighted.
SO.11
DC Cable Sizing
DC-side voltage drop for a given cable size and run length, plus the minimum cable cross-section for a target drop percentage.
SO.12
Charge Controller
PWM controller current rating and MPPT max input voltage / output current checks, from array Isc, Voc and power.
SO.15
Battery SOC Simulator
Live 24-hour state-of-charge curve from array power, sun hours, daily load and starting SOC — see the minimum SOC reached against your floor and where the day ends up.
SO.13
Row Spacing
Minimum row-to-row spacing to avoid self-shading at winter solstice, plus row pitch and ground coverage ratio.
SO.14
CO2 Offset
Annual and lifetime CO2 emissions avoided by a solar system, from production and a grid emission factor, plus everyday equivalents.
SO.16
Inverter Sizing
Continuous and motor-starting surge sizing for off-grid/hybrid inverters, plus a DC:AC oversizing ratio check for grid-tie systems.
SO.02
Learning Notes
Reference-only theory notes on PV fundamentals, system design and sizing concepts, storage and battery chemistry, and solar economics and maintenance — not a calculator.
SO.08
Symbol Library
Click-to-explore reference of common PV single-line diagram symbols — modules and arrays, inverters, wiring and connections, battery storage, metering and safety devices.
SO.09
Engineering Guides
Ten step-by-step how-to guides — array sizing, battery sizing, inverter DC:AC ratio, reading a module datasheet, string voltage, system losses, grounding and more.
SO.10
Comparisons
Eight head-to-head comparisons — mono vs poly vs thin-film cells, string vs micro inverters, lead-acid vs lithium batteries, and more.
