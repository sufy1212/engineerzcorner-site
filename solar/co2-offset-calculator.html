CO2 Offset & Environmental Savings Calculator

Annual and lifetime emissions avoided, from production and a grid emission factor
Solar Index SO.01 Sizing Tools SO.14 CO2 Offset SO.02 Notes
Every kWh generated by a solar system is a kWh that (mostly) didn't have to come from the grid — and grid electricity almost everywhere still carries some carbon cost. This converts annual production into avoided CO2, both per year and over a typical system lifetime.
