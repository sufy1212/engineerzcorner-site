Charge Controller Sizing Calculator

PWM current rating and MPPT voltage/current checks, side by side
Solar Index SO.01 Sizing Tools SO.12 Charge Controller SO.02 Notes
PWM and MPPT controllers are sized differently — PWM is a straightforward current check against the array's short-circuit current, while MPPT also needs the array's cold-weather Voc checked against the controller's maximum input voltage rating. Use whichever step matches your controller type.

PWM Controller Sizing

PWM controllers connect the array near-directly to the battery bank, so they're sized on current alone, with a standard safety margin for the array's short-circuit current.

PWM controllers need the array's nominal voltage to roughly match the battery bank voltage — they can't step voltage down.

MPPT Controller Sizing

MPPT controllers can accept a higher array voltage than the battery bank and convert it down, so they're checked on both maximum input voltage and output current.
