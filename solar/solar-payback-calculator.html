A simple-payback view, not a discounted cash flow — it ignores financing cost and the time value of money.

How this is calculated
Each year: Savings = production × (1 − degradation)^(yr−1) × rate × (1 + escalation)^(yr−1) − O&M  ·  Payback: the year cumulative savings first exceeds net system cost  ·  ROI: (lifetime savings − net cost) / net cost × 100
Ignores financing interest, the time value of money, and any inverter replacement cost partway through the system's life — build those into O&M or the net cost if they apply to your case. For a discounted view, a real financial model with NPV/IRR is more appropriate.
Solar payback — a simple-payback budgeting estimate, not financial advice. Actual savings depend on your utility's rate structure (net metering vs. net billing), local incentive rules and real production. ← Solar Index
