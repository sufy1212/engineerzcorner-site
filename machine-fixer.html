About Machine Fixer

You're given a symptom — a pump that vibrates and rattles, a motor running hot — and a small diagram of the equipment train it belongs to. One part in that diagram is drawn as failed: worn, seized, cracked, misaligned, clogged, or leaking. Matching the symptom to the right part is the core skill the game is testing.

Across 30 machines, the scenarios span rotating equipment, fluid handling, and mechanical power transmission, so the same-sounding symptom can trace back to a different root cause each time. It's a quick way to build pattern recognition for common mechanical failure modes rather than a full repair guide.
