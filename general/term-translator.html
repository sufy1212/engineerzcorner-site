About This Glossary

This is a glossary of common jobsite and safety terms translated across five languages, meant to help a multilingual crew communicate quickly on common instructions, hazard warnings, and everyday site vocabulary.

It's deliberately scoped to quick, everyday communication — for anything safety-critical, contractual, or legally significant, such as permits, incident reports, or formal training, use a qualified professional interpreter instead. A glossary can't carry the context, tone, or legal nuance that situation requires.
