Three Common Percentage Questions

This covers three distinct calculations that often get blurred together: finding X% of a value (what's 15% of 340), finding what percent one number is of another (45 is what percent of 300), and increasing or decreasing a value by a percentage (a value after a 12% increase). The second and third in particular are easy to confuse, since both involve two numbers and a percentage but answer different questions.

Typical uses: sanity-checking a discount or markup, calculating a tax add-on, or verifying that a percentage change someone reported actually matches the underlying numbers — without reaching for a spreadsheet for a single quick check.

Straightforward percentage math — not tax, VAT, or currency-specific advice.
