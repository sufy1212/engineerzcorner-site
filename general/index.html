General Tools — Index

Fourteen standalone utilities that aren't specific to any one engineering discipline — unit conversion, a site term translator, date math, markup/margin, a fraction-decimal converter for tape-measure work, a number-base converter, a percentage calculator, a time-duration calculator, a BMI calculator, an age calculator, a tip calculator, a password generator, an engineering sanity-checker, and a text case converter. Pick one below.
GT.01
Unit Converter
Length, mass, area, volume, pressure, temperature, force, torque, energy, power and speed — convert live between units in each category.
GT.02
Site Term Translator
English site & safety vocabulary translated into Spanish, French, Portuguese, German and Polish — a curated glossary, not machine translation.
GT.03
Date & Business-Day Calculator
Add or subtract days from a date, or find the total days, weeks, and weekdays (Mon–Fri) between two dates.
GT.04
Markup, Margin & % Change
Cost/sell price to markup % and margin %, plus a plain old-value/new-value percentage-change calculator.
GT.05
Fraction ↔ Decimal (Feet-Inches)
Feet-inches-fraction (like a tape measure reads) to decimal feet/inches/mm, and back — snapped to the nearest 1/16".
GT.06
Number Base Converter
Decimal, hexadecimal, binary and octal — enter a value in any base and see it in the other three at once.
GT.07
Percentage Calculator
X% of Y, X is what % of Y, and increase/decrease a value by a percentage — three quick everyday percentage tools in one place.
GT.08
Time Duration Calculator
Elapsed time between two clock times (handles crossing midnight), or add/subtract a duration to a starting time — for shifts, timesheets and scheduling.
GT.09
BMI Calculator
Body mass index from metric (kg/cm) or imperial (lb/ft-in) inputs, with the standard WHO weight-category bands.
GT.10
Age Calculator
Exact age in years/months/days from a date of birth, total days lived, and a countdown to the next birthday.
GT.11
Tip Calculator
Tip amount and total from a bill and tip %, split evenly across any number of people.
GT.12
Password Generator
Random passwords generated locally with crypto.getRandomValues — length and character sets configurable, nothing sent over the network.
GT.13
Engineering Sanity-Checker
Enter a value and unit for common engineering quantities (stress, pressure, voltage, torque and more) — flags whether it's a plausible order of magnitude and catches likely unit-slip mistakes.
GT.14
Text Case Converter
UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title, Sentence, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE — all live, with a copy button on each.
