Calendar Days vs. Business Days

Calendar-day math counts every day in a range; business-day math skips weekends. This matters whenever a contract specifies a term like "10 business days" for a submittal or payment — the actual calendar date that falls on depends entirely on how many weekends land inside the window, so calendar-day and business-day counts diverge quickly on longer spans.

Two modes are available: add or subtract a number of days from a given date (for example, "45 days from today"), or count the number of days between two given dates — useful for verifying a schedule duration or checking exactly how many days remain before a deadline.

Weekday count treats Saturday and Sunday as non-working days only — it doesn't know about public holidays.
