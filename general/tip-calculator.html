How the Split Is Calculated

The tip amount is the bill multiplied by the tip percentage, and the total is the bill plus that tip. Dividing both by the number of people gives two different useful numbers: the total each person owes if splitting the whole bill evenly, and just their share of the tip if everyone already covered their own food and only wants to split the gratuity.

This is an even split by design — it divides the bill and tip equally across however many people you enter, regardless of who ordered what. If the table's orders were very different in cost, an even split may not feel fair, and you'd want to calculate each person's tip based on their own item total instead.

Splits the tip and bill evenly across the number of people entered — doesn't account for uneven orders.
