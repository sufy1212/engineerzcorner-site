Markup vs. Margin — Not the Same Number

Markup is the percentage added on top of cost to reach a selling price; margin is the percentage of that selling price which ends up as profit. They're calculated from different bases (cost vs. price), so they're never the same number for the same transaction — a 50% markup on a $100 cost gives a $150 price, but the resulting margin is only 33.3%, not 50%. Confusing the two is one of the most common pricing mistakes.
