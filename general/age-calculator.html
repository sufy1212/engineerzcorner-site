Exact Age, Not Just a Round Number

The calculation walks the calendar day by day between the two dates rather than dividing total days by 365, so it correctly accounts for variable month lengths and leap years — a rough days/365 estimate can be off by a day or more depending on exactly which date range it spans.

The optional "as of" date lets you calculate someone's exact age at any point in time, not just today — useful for confirming an exact age at a past event, checking eligibility against an age-based cutoff, or working out how many days remain until a specific birthday.
