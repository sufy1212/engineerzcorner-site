How this is calculated
Rankine active pressure coefficient: Ka = (1 − sinφ) / (1 + sinφ)
Active force: Pa = ½ × Ka × γ × H², acting at H/3 above the base  ·  Overturning moment: Mo = Pa × H/3  ·  Resisting moment (about toe): Mr = W × B/2
FOS(overturning) = Mr / Mo  ·  FOS(sliding) = μW / Pa
Weight centroid is simplified to B/2 from the toe and passive resistance in front of the wall is ignored entirely — both conservative simplifications. A full design also checks bearing pressure under the base (eccentricity/toe pressure) and the wall's internal bending and shear capacity.
Simplified Rankine method — verify against Eurocode 7 / local geotechnical code and a full geotechnical report for a real wall.
