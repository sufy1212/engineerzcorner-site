About the Grade / Slope Calculator

Enter a rise and run — or a known slope percentage or angle — and it converts between the common ways grade gets expressed on drawings and in the field: percent grade, ratio (like 1:20), and degrees from horizontal. It's the kind of quick conversion that comes up constantly when reading a civil drawing against a field measurement, or checking a spec written in one format against a survey given in another.

A positive rise is treated as uphill in the direction of travel, so a downhill grade should be entered as a negative rise — that sign convention matters if you're chaining this into a longer profile calculation. This tool converts between representations of a given slope; it doesn't check that slope against a code-required maximum, which varies by application (ramps, roads, drainage) and jurisdiction.
