How this is calculated
UDL: M = wL²/8, δ = 5wL⁴/(384EI)  ·  Point load at midspan: M = PL/4, δ = PL³/(48EI)
Bending stress: σ = M/Z  ·  Method: elastic (working-stress) beam theory — standard for a first-pass serviceability and strength check.
This checks bending and deflection only — shear capacity, lateral-torsional buckling and bearing at supports all need separate checks for a real design.
Practical sizing check — verify against ACI 318 / Eurocode 2-3 / AISC 360 (as applicable) for anything load-bearing.
