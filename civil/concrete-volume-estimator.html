How this is calculated
Wet volume: V = L × W × T × qty × (1 + wastage%)  ·  Dry volume: Vd = V × 1.54
Material volume: Vmaterial = Vd × (part / sum of parts)  ·  Cement bags = cement volume ÷ 0.035 m³ (one 50kg bag)
The 1.54 dry-volume factor and bag weight are common rule-of-thumb figures — actual yield varies with aggregate grading, moisture content and compaction. Always confirm against the specific mix design and supplier data for procurement quantities.
Quick take-off estimate only — a full mix design accounts for strength grade, water-cement ratio and admixtures.
