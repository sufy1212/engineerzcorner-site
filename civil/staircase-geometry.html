Splits a floor-to-floor height into a whole number of even risers, then checks the resulting riser/tread combination against the common 2R+T comfort rule. Doesn't check headroom, handrail requirements, or your local building code's specific riser/tread limits — those vary by jurisdiction and occupancy.

How this is calculated
n_risers = round(rise / target riser)  ·  actual riser = rise / n_risers  ·  2R + T target ≈ 550–700mm
The 2R+T (or "rule of thumb") comfort check is a widely used design guideline, not a universal code requirement — always confirm against your specific building code's riser/tread/headroom rules.
Geometry rule-of-thumb only — headroom, handrails and code-specific riser/tread limits need a full stair design check.
