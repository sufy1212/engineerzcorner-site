How this is calculated
Q = (1/n)·A·R^(2/3)·S^(1/2)  ·  A = π/4·D²  ·  R = D/4 (full pipe)
This is the full-flow (100% depth) capacity — actual design flow is normally checked against 80–90% of full-flow capacity to leave freeboard, and partial-flow depth/velocity differs from these full-pipe values (use a d/D ratio chart for part-full conditions).
Gravity flow, uniform (normal-depth) conditions assumed — not valid for pressurized or surcharged pipe.
