Tension Development Length

Common metric sizes: 10, 12, 16, 20, 25, 32mm.
DEVELOPMENT LENGTH, ld —
How this is calculated
ld = db·fy·ψt·ψe / (factor·λ·√f'c)  [fy, f'c in psi; db, ld in inches]
factor = 25 for db ≤ 19mm (#6)  ·  factor = 20 for db ≥ 22mm (#7)
Minimum ld = 300mm (12in). Your MPa/mm inputs are converted internally.
This is the ACI 318 Case 1 simplified table value — it assumes clear spacing ≥ db and clear cover ≥ db (or equivalent minimum stirrups). Tighter spacing/cover, or Case 2 conditions, require the full confinement-term equation (25.4.2.4), not this simplified table.
