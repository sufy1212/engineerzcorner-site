How this is calculated
Total design load: Ntotal = N × (1 + allowance%)  ·  Required area: A = Ntotal / qa  ·  Square side: B = √A, rounded up
Method: working-stress bearing check against allowable soil pressure — the standard first-pass method for isolated spread footings on reasonable soil.
This doesn't check settlement, punching shear, one-way shear, or reinforcement — all required for a complete footing design, and settlement can govern over bearing capacity on soft or compressible soils.
Practical sizing method — verify against a site-specific geotechnical report and ACI 318 / Eurocode 7 for a complete design.
