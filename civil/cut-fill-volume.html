About the Cut/Fill Volume Calculator

Given the cross-sectional cut and fill areas at two adjacent stations along an alignment, plus the distance between them, this applies the average end area method to estimate the earthwork volume in that interval. It's the standard hand-calc approach for a quick quantity check on a road, pad, or trench alignment before turning to full 3D surface modeling.

One interval on its own only gives a partial picture — for a total cut or fill quantity across a full alignment, run this same calculation across every station-pair interval and sum the results. The average end area method is an approximation that works well for fairly uniform ground; a surface with sharp local variation between stations is better served by a full surface-to-surface volume calculation.
