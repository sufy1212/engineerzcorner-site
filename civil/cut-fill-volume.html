How this is calculated
V = (A1 + A2) / 2 × L
Average end area slightly overestimates volume on convex (prismoidal) sections — for high-accuracy earthwork on major grading jobs, use the prismoidal formula or a surface-model (DTM) takeoff instead. This method is standard for quick manual quantity checks.
Sum this result across every station-pair interval along the alignment for a total cut or fill quantity.
