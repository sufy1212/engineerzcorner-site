About the Slab-on-Grade Thickness Calculator

Enter the loading a slab needs to carry and basic subgrade support information, and this returns a preliminary thickness estimate — useful for early-stage budgeting, comparing options, or sanity-checking a thickness that's already been proposed before a full structural design is run.

It's a preliminary figure rather than a stamped design value. Final slab thickness depends on the subgrade's actual modulus of subgrade reaction (from geotechnical testing, not an assumed value), the specific loading pattern and load factors that apply, joint layout, and reinforcement — all of which belong in a proper structural slab design rather than this quick estimate.
