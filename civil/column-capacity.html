How this is calculated
Pcr = π²EI / Le²  ·  r = √(I/A)  ·  λ = Le/r  ·  Allowable = Pcr / FOS
Method: classical Euler buckling — most accurate for slender columns (λ typically >100-120); short, stocky columns are usually governed by material crushing/yielding instead, which this sheet doesn't check.
λ above ~200 is impractically slender for most codes. For reinforced concrete columns, use the code's own interaction-diagram method instead — Euler buckling alone doesn't capture concrete's behavior well.
Classical elastic buckling check — verify against AISC 360 / Eurocode 3 (steel) or ACI 318 / Eurocode 2 (concrete) for a real design.
