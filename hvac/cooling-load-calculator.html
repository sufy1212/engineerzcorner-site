Sensible + Latent Cooling Load

Supply airflow and temperature/humidity drop → total cooling duty
HVAC Index HV.01 Duct Sizing HV.02 Cooling Load HV.03 Chiller Capacity HV.04 Notes
A cooling load is the sum of sensible heat (temperature) and latent heat (moisture) that has to be removed. Enter airflow, the temperature drop across the coil, and the humidity ratio drop, and this splits the total into both parts.

Airflow & Sensible Load

Room/return temperature minus supply air temperature off the coil.
2

Latent Load

Moisture content removed — return air humidity ratio minus supply air humidity ratio. Get both off a psychrometric chart for your actual conditions; 2–5 g/kg is a typical range for comfort cooling in a humid climate.

How this is calculated
Qsensible = 1.005 · ρ · Qairflow · ΔT  (kW)
Qlatent = 2501 · ρ · Qairflow · Δw / 1000  (kW, using Δw in g/kg)
SHR = Qsensible / (Qsensible + Qlatent)  ·  1 ton of refrigeration = 3.517 kW
Uses standard specific heat and latent heat of vaporization constants for moist air at typical comfort conditions — a full psychrometric calculation accounts for more, but this is the same core method most quick load estimates use.
Covers the airside cooling duty only — doesn't include envelope/solar/occupancy loads, which get summed in separately in a full room-by-room load calculation (Mechanical's Sensible Heat Load sheet covers the envelope side).
