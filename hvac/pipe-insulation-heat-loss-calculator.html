Pipe Insulation Heat Loss

Radial conduction through a cylindrical insulation layer
Steady-state conductive heat loss (or gain) through a cylindrical insulation layer around a pipe — useful for comparing insulation thickness options or estimating standing losses. Ignores surface film resistance (convection at the outer surface) for simplicity, which makes this a conservative (slightly high) estimate of heat loss.
