About the Pipe Insulation Heat Loss Calculator

Given a pipe's operating temperature, its insulation thickness and conductivity, and the surrounding air temperature, this works out steady-state radial heat loss per unit length through the insulation. It's a quick way to compare how much difference a thicker layer or a different insulation material makes on a given line, or to sanity-check a spec before it's finalized.

Because it doesn't include the outer surface film coefficient, actual heat loss to still air will run somewhat lower than what this returns — treat the result as a conservative, comparative figure rather than a precise field measurement. A full insulation thickness design also needs to weigh surface condensation risk on cold lines and the economic thickness that balances insulation cost against ongoing energy loss, neither of which this calculator covers.
