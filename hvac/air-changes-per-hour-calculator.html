Air Changes per Hour

Room ventilation rate from volume and supply airflow
Air changes per hour (ACH) measures how many times the full air volume of a room is replaced by the ventilation system in one hour. Enter the room dimensions and the supply (or extract) airflow to check the ACH you're actually delivering, then use the reverse calc to size airflow for a target ACH.

How this is calculated
ACH = Airflow (m³/h) / Room volume (m³)
Assumes reasonably even mixing — pockets of stagnant air near the floor or behind obstructions won't get the full nominal ACH even when the bulk average checks out.
