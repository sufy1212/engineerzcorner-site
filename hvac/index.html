HVAC — Calculator Index

Fig. H1 — Fan, damper and diffuser, one duct run
Eight standalone HVAC calculators plus a learning-notes reference page — pick one below. These are HVAC-specific: duct/airflow sizing, cooling load, refrigeration capacity, sensible heat and pipe insulation — general mechanical topics like pumps and piping live under the Mechanical discipline instead.
HV.01
Duct Sizing & Air Changes
Required airflow from room volume and air-change rate (or entered directly), sized into a circular or rectangular duct at a target velocity.
HV.02
Sensible + Latent Cooling Load
Sensible load from supply airflow and temperature drop, latent load from moisture removed, and the combined total cooling capacity needed.
HV.03
Refrigeration / Chiller Capacity
Compressor input power and condenser heat rejection from a cooling duty and COP, plus a refrigerant-side mass flow estimate.
HV.11
Heat Pump Balance Point
The outdoor temperature where heat pump output stops covering building heat loss, plus backup heat sizing at design temperature.
 Simulator
HV.10
Psychrometric Chart Simulator
Drag sliders for dry-bulb temperature, relative humidity and altitude — watch the state point, humidity ratio, wet-bulb, dew point and enthalpy update live.
HV.07
Symbol Library
Click-to-explore reference of common HVAC ductwork and equipment symbols.
HV.08
Engineering Guides
Ten step-by-step how-to guides — cooling/heating load calculations, duct sizing, AHU selection, psychrometrics and more.
HV.09
Comparisons
Eight head-to-head equipment comparisons — VAV vs CAV, chilled water vs DX, RTU vs split vs VRF, and more.
HV.05
Sensible Heat / Supply Airflow
Required supply CFM to offset a sensible cooling load at a chosen supply-air ΔT.
HV.06
Pipe Insulation Heat Loss
Radial conduction heat loss through a cylindrical pipe insulation layer.
HV.07
Air Changes per Hour
Room ACH from volume and airflow, plus the airflow needed to hit a target ACH.
HV.12
Refrigerant Charge (Superheat / Subcooling)
Superheat method for fixed orifice/cap tube, or subcooling method for TXV/EEV — actual value vs target, with an undercharged/overcharged check.
HV.13
SEER / EER / COP Converter
Exact COP ↔ EER unit conversion, plus an approximate SEER/SEER2 relationship — enter any one, get the other two.
HV.14
Fan Static & Total Pressure
FSP and FTP from inlet/outlet duct static pressure and velocity readings, per the standard AMCA field-test relationship.
HV.04
Learning Notes
Reference-only theory notes on psychrometrics, air distribution, heating & cooling equipment, controls, indoor air quality, energy, standards and safety — not a calculator.
