How this is calculated
Saturation vapor pressure (Magnus-Tetens): Psat = 610.94·exp(17.625T / (T+243.04)) Pa, T in °C.
Atmospheric pressure by altitude: Patm = 101325·(1 − 2.25577×10⁻⁵h)5.2559 Pa.
Vapor pressure: Pv = (RH/100)·Psat. Humidity ratio: W = 0.622·Pv / (Patm − Pv).
Dew point: inverse of the Magnus formula from Pv. Wet-bulb: Stull (2011) empirical approximation from T and RH.
Enthalpy: h = 1.006T + W·(2501 + 1.805T) kJ/kg dry air.
Magnus-Tetens and Stull approximations are accurate to a few tenths of a degree/percent across normal comfort and process ranges — good for teaching and quick checks, not a replacement for ASHRAE psychrometric tables on contractual work.
