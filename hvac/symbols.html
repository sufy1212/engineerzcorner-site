Full HVAC Symbol Reference

Every symbol above, with its full meaning and typical usage in one scannable table — useful for a quick page search or side-by-side comparison instead of clicking through each tile individually.

SymbolCategoryMeaningWhere it's used
Supply Air DiffuserAir DistributionA square outline with a crossed X — the standard plan symbol for a 4-way ceiling supply diffuser.Marked on reflected ceiling plans wherever conditioned supply air enters a room.
Return Air GrilleAir DistributionA square outline with horizontal slats — the plan symbol for a return or exhaust air grille.Marked wherever room air is drawn back into the return-air duct system.
DamperAir DistributionA rectangle across the duct with an internal blade line — regulates or shuts off airflow at that point.Volume control, fire, smoke and balancing dampers shown in-line on ductwork drawings.
Air Handling UnitEquipmentA rectangular casing outline with a through airflow line — the generic AHU/packaged unit symbol.Mechanical room and roof plans wherever a central air handling unit sits in the airstream.
Centrifugal FanEquipmentA circular scroll housing with an internal curved blade line — represents a centrifugal supply/exhaust fan.Standalone exhaust fans, supply fans and fan sections within an AHU.
Cooling CoilEquipmentA repeating wave/zigzag line representing finned coil tubing in the airstream.Chilled-water or DX cooling coils inside an AHU — the same base symbol is reused for heating coils, distinguished by label (CC/HC).
Heating CoilEquipmentThe same finned-coil wave symbol as a cooling coil — the label (HC vs CC) is what distinguishes function, not the shape.Hot-water or electric heating coils inside an AHU or in a duct-mounted reheat section.
ThermostatControlsA circle enclosing the letter T — the generic temperature sensing/control device symbol.Wall-mounted room thermostats and duct-mounted temperature sensors on control drawings.
VAV BoxEquipmentA divided rectangular casing — represents a variable-air-volume terminal unit modulating airflow to a zone.Above-ceiling zone airflow control on VAV air distribution system drawings.
Duct ElbowDuctworkA smooth curved duct section — represents a 90° turn in the ductwork run.Wherever a duct run changes direction on a ductwork layout drawing.
Exhaust Air GrilleAir DistributionA slatted grille outline with a dashed centerline — removes stale or contaminated air directly to outside, not recirculated.Kitchens, toilets and any space needing dedicated exhaust rather than general return air.
LouverAir DistributionA weather-resistant slatted opening — the wall or roof penetration point where outside air enters or leaves the building.Fresh air intakes and relief air outlets shown on building elevations and mechanical schedules.
Fire DamperAir DistributionA damper symbol labeled FD — automatically closes on fire detection to maintain a fire-rated barrier's integrity.Every point ductwork penetrates a fire-rated wall or floor assembly.
Smoke DamperAir DistributionA damper symbol labeled SD — closes on smoke detection to control smoke migration through the duct system.Smoke control zones and duct penetrations of smoke barriers on life-safety drawings.
Linear DiffuserAir DistributionA narrow slot diffuser — delivers supply air along a continuous line rather than a single ceiling point.Perimeter zones and architectural ceilings where a continuous linear air distribution look is wanted.
ChillerEquipmentA three-section casing outline — produces chilled water by mechanical refrigeration for distribution to cooling coils.Central plant drawings showing the chilled-water source feeding the building's cooling coils.
BoilerEquipmentA casing with internal flame/heat lines — produces hot water or steam by combustion for heating distribution.Central plant drawings showing the heating hot-water or steam source feeding heating coils.
Split AC UnitEquipmentA wall-mounted indoor unit outline — a self-contained cooling unit paired with an outdoor condensing unit.Small rooms and standalone spaces served by dedicated split-system cooling rather than central air.
Fan Coil UnitEquipmentA compact casing with an internal coil and fan — a local terminal unit conditioning air with chilled/hot water from a central plant.Perimeter zones and hotel/apartment rooms needing individual zone control from a central hydronic system.
HumidifierEquipmentA casing with an internal moisture-wave line — adds water vapor to the supply airstream to raise humidity.AHU sections and duct-mounted units serving spaces with a minimum humidity requirement.
Energy Recovery VentilatorEquipmentA divided casing with two airstreams crossing — transfers heat/moisture between exhaust and incoming fresh air.Fresh-air makeup systems recovering energy from exhaust air before it's discarded, cutting conditioning load.
Rooftop Package UnitEquipmentA large enclosure symbol representing a factory-packaged, self-contained rooftop AC unit.Roof plans for commercial buildings served by packaged rooftop units rather than a central plant.
Duct TransitionDuctworkA tapered duct section — changes duct size or shape between two different cross-sections.Ductwork layout drawings wherever duct dimensions step up or down along a run.
Flexible DuctDuctworkA wavy line representing corrugated flexible ductwork — a short, flexible connection rather than rigid sheet metal.Final connections from rigid duct to a diffuser or grille where minor alignment flexibility is needed.
Duct TeeDuctworkA branch duct splitting from a main run at a right angle — the standard T-shaped ductwork junction.Ductwork layout drawings at every point a branch duct is taken off a main trunk.
Duct Access DoorDuctworkA hinged panel set into a duct wall — provides access for inspection, cleaning or fire damper reset.Ductwork drawings at fire dampers, coils and any point requiring periodic internal access.
HumidistatControlsA circle enclosing the letter H — the generic humidity sensing/control device symbol.Wall or duct-mounted humidity control points on control drawings.
CO2 SensorControlsAn enclosure labeled CO2 — measures indoor carbon dioxide concentration to drive demand-controlled ventilation.Densely occupied spaces where outside air rate is modulated based on measured occupancy load.
Duct Pressure SensorControlsA circle enclosing the letter P — measures static or differential pressure for fan and damper control.Duct static pressure control points and filter differential pressure monitoring.
Damper ActuatorControlsA boxed cross symbol mounted on a damper — automatically drives the damper blade position from a control signal.Every automatically controlled damper on a VAV, economizer or zone control drawing.
Duct FilterDuctworkA rectangular frame with a dashed media pattern — removes particulates from the airstream.AHU filter sections and duct-mounted filter boxes on air handling drawings.
Sound AttenuatorDuctworkA duct section with internal baffle lines — reduces fan and airflow noise transmitted through the ductwork.Fan discharge and inlet ducts, and runs serving noise-sensitive occupied spaces.
Expansion TankEquipmentA closed cylindrical vessel — absorbs the volume change of a hydronic system's water as it heats and cools.Closed chilled-water and heating-water hydronic loops, typically near the system's pump.
Air SeparatorEquipmentA cylindrical vessel in-line on the piping — removes entrained air from circulating hydronic water.Hydronic piping systems near the expansion tank, preventing air-related pump cavitation and noise.
