How this is calculated
Airflow (m³/s) = Qs / (1.2 × 1.005 × ΔT)
Uses standard air density (1.2 kg/m³) and specific heat (1.005 kJ/kg·K) at sea level — correct for altitude if the site is significantly above sea level, since air density (and this relationship) changes with it.

About the Sensible Heat Airflow Calculator

Given a sensible heat load and the supply-to-room temperature difference you're designing to, this works out the airflow needed to carry that load away. It's the same relationship behind sizing a diffuser or checking whether a proposed supply air quantity can actually handle the sensible load in a space.

It only covers the sensible side of the load — spaces with a meaningful latent component (kitchens, gyms, densely occupied rooms) need that checked separately, since sensible airflow alone won't tell you whether dehumidification is adequate. And the airflow this calculator returns is a minimum for sensible cooling — code-minimum ventilation rates can end up governing the actual supply airflow instead, so it's worth checking both.
