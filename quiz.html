About the Daily Quiz

Five multiple-choice questions, pulled from a bank spanning Electrical, Mechanical, HVAC, Civil, Automation, General, and Project Management — covering fundamentals like control theory, fluid mechanics, reinforced concrete, RACI roles, and earned value, rather than trivia. Each question includes a short explanation once you answer, so a wrong guess still teaches the underlying concept.

The set refreshes daily, so it's meant as a quick recurring warm-up rather than a one-time test — five questions is short enough to fit into a coffee break, and the mix of disciplines means you'll periodically get quizzed on something outside your main specialty.

There's no login or account required — your score for the day is tracked locally and you can share the result if you want to compare with a colleague. If a question touches on something you'd rather review properly than just recall, the discipline notes cover the underlying formulas and reference tables in more depth, and the symbol flashcards are a good companion for the drawing-standard side of things.
