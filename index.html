Calendar & Date Converter

Pick any date (or click a day in the month view) to see it in three systems. Hijri uses the standard calculated/tabular Islamic calendar — it's a fixed arithmetic rule, not a moon sighting, so it can run a day or two ahead of or behind the date your local moon-sighting authority announces. Korean shows both the Dangi era year (the traditional Korean calendar era, still year-counted alongside the Gregorian calendar) and the date written in Korean year-month-day order — Korea's civil calendar has been Gregorian since 1896, so this isn't a separate day-to-day calendar, just how the same date is written and era-numbered there.
