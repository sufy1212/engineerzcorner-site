About Lux Planner

The map uses a simplified lighting model: each fitting is a diffuse point source, light falls with distance and angle onto the work plane, and the result is scaled by a 0.8 maintenance factor with a 20% allowance for reflected light. It shows why spacing and mounting height matter, but it is not a photometric calculation. Real designs use the manufacturer's data.

The targets come from typical EN 12464-1 values. For the method behind them see Lighting Design Beyond Lux and the lighting calculator.
