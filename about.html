Engineering Studies Toolkit

About This Toolkit

What's here, how to use it, and where its limits are.
A free set of reference calculators across eight engineering disciplines, built for quick sanity checks and first-pass sizing — not a substitute for a stamped calculation package.

What's here

Electrical (short circuit, voltage drop, selectivity/TCC, cable sizing, panel estimate, transformer/genset sizing, busbar/enclosure sizing), Mechanical (pipe flow, pump power, duct sizing, heat load, belt drive), HVAC (duct sizing, cooling load, refrigeration/chiller capacity), Civil (beam bending, column capacity, footing sizing, retaining wall stability), Automation (signal scaling, PID tuning, control valve sizing, level transmitter ranging, VFD speed scaling, plus a symbol library, engineering guides and comparisons), Solar (array/battery/inverter sizing, plus a symbol library, engineering guides and comparisons), Project Management (RACI matrix, risk register, action/task tracker, meeting minutes, earned value management, team directory, punch list, permit-to-work/LOTO, plus commercial terms, Incoterms and PMBOK/PMP reference pages), and general-purpose tools (unit converter, site term translator, date/business-day calculator, markup/margin, fraction/decimal, number base converter). Most disciplines also have a learning-notes reference page covering the underlying theory.

Alongside the engineering disciplines, a separate Study Abroad section covers the practical side of studying overseas — IELTS/PTE/TOEFL score conversion, cost of living, education loan EMI, a simplified visa points estimate, university fee comparison, currency conversion, an SOP word checker, and an application timeline planner. It isn't engineering content and isn't held to the same code-reference standard as the calculators above — see the Terms & Disclaimer for its specific caveats.

There's also an Interview Prep section with discipline-specific and general/behavioral questions and model-answer talking points, a growing Blog of short technical articles, and a set of free browser games under Technical Games — Quickfire (calculation speed drills), the Daily Engineering Quiz, Symbol Flashcards, plus visual troubleshooting games (Fault Finder, Defect Finder, Machine Fixer, Signal Chain). All games are free with no signup required.

How to use it

Every calculator is self-contained on its own page — fill in the inputs, read the result. None of the figures here are a substitute for your own engineering judgement, applicable codes and standards, or a qualified reviewer's sign-off.
