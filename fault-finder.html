About Fault Finder

Every round gives you a symptom in plain language — a motor that won't start, a circuit that trips repeatedly — alongside a small single-line diagram of that circuit. One component in the diagram is drawn faulty: blown, tripped, burnt, reversed, loose, or broken. Reading the symptom and matching it to the right point in the circuit is the whole game.

The 12 circuits cover common electrical fault patterns rather than one repeated layout, so the same symptom can point to a different culprit depending on the diagram in front of you. It's meant as a quick way to sharpen symptom-to-cause reasoning, not a substitute for hands-on troubleshooting training.
