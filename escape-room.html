About the Engineering Escape Room

Each scenario locks five doors behind a real engineering question, drawn from a mix of disciplines rather than a single specialty. One shared clock runs for the whole room, so a wrong answer doesn't cost a life — it costs time, and the door stays shut until you get it right.

The eliminate-one hint trims a wrong option for a time penalty if you're stuck, but otherwise it's a straight race against the clock across all five doors. It's built to feel like a puzzle room rather than a quiz, testing whether you can work fast under pressure as much as whether you know the material.
