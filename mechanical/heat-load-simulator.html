How this is calculated
People: 130 W/person (sensible + latent, typical office activity).
Equipment / Lighting: entered W/m² × floor area.
Solar gain: glazing area × solar gain factor (W/m²), reduced by any shading you build into the factor.
Transmission: U × Envelope area × (Tout − Tin).
Fresh air (sensible): 1.2 × (L/s per person × occupants) × (Tout − Tin) watts.
Total = sum of sources × (1 + safety margin). Tons = kW / 3.517. BTU/hr = kW × 3412.
Simplified single design-point ASHRAE-style rule-of-thumb model — no latent-load split, hour-by-hour solar profile, or infiltration term. For a full load calculation use Manual J / CLTD methods or dedicated HVAC load software.
