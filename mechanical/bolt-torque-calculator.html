How this is calculated
Proof load: Fp = As × proof stress  ·  Target clamp force: F = Fp × target%  ·  Torque: T = K × D × F
The short-form K-factor method is a widely used approximation — actual torque-tension relationship is sensitive to friction under the head/nut and in the threads, which varies with plating, lubrication and reuse. For critical or fatigue joints, use the fastener manufacturer's published torque values.
