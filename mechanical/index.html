Mechanical — Calculator Index

Fig. M1 — Pump, valve and tank, one loop
Eight standalone mechanical calculators plus guides, comparisons, a symbol library and a learning-notes reference page — pick one below. These aren't chained together (a pipe run and a belt drive aren't naturally part of one system), so each one is self-contained.
MH.01
Pipe Flow & Head Loss
Velocity, Reynolds number and friction head loss for flow in a pipe, via Darcy-Weisbach with the Swamee-Jain friction factor.
MH.02
Pump Power & NPSH
Hydraulic, shaft and motor input power from flow and head, with a recommended standard motor size and an NPSH cavitation-margin check.
MH.03
Duct Sizing & Air Changes
Required airflow from room volume and air-change rate (or entered directly), sized into a circular or rectangular duct at a target velocity.
MH.04
Sensible Heat Load
Cooling/heating load from supply airflow and temperature difference, or a quick room estimate from floor area and a W/m² rule of thumb.
MH.05
Belt / Pulley Drive
Driven-pulley speed, belt pitch-line speed and shaft torques from pulley diameters, driver speed and transmitted power.
MH.06
Pump / Fan Affinity Laws
Scale flow, head and power to a new speed for a centrifugal pump or fan.
MH.07
Thermal Expansion of Piping
Free linear growth of a pipe run between anchors, by material and temperature change.
MH.10
Fastener Torque
Tightening torque for a target bolt preload, by grade and nut factor.
 Simulator
MH.11
Heat Load Simulator
Drag sliders for occupancy, floor area and outdoor design temperature — watch the load breakdown and tonnage update live.
 Simulator
MH.12
Pump System Curve Simulator
Drag sliders for VFD speed, static head and system friction — watch the pump/system curve intersection, flow, head and power update live.
 Simulator
MH.13
Belt & Pulley Drive Simulator
Watch two pulleys spin live as you drag diameters, driver RPM and load — driven RPM updates in real time and the belt visibly slips past capacity.
MH.14
Bearing L10 Life
Basic (and reliability/lubrication-modified) rating life in hours from dynamic load rating, equivalent load and speed.
MH.15
Shaft Diameter Sizing
Minimum solid shaft diameter under combined bending and torsion, using the ASME combined-shock-and-fatigue design equation.
MH.08
Engineering Guides
Twelve step-by-step how-to guides — pump sizing, pipe head loss, belt drives, NPSH, heat exchangers and more.
MH.09
Comparisons
Ten head-to-head equipment comparisons — pump types, belts vs chains vs gears, bearing types, valve types, refrigerants and more.
MH.08
Symbol Library
Click-to-explore reference of common P&ID and mechanical drawing symbols.
MH.06
Learning Notes
Reference-only theory notes on mechanical fundamentals, fluids, thermodynamics, machine design, materials, rotating equipment, standards and maintenance — not a calculator.
