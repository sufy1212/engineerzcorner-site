About the Thermal Expansion Calculator

Enter a pipe run's length, its material, and the temperature change it will see in service, and this returns how much that run grows or shrinks in free (unrestrained) expansion. It's the starting figure behind deciding whether a straight run needs an expansion loop, joint, or offset — a run that barely moves might not need one, while a long high-temperature line often will.

This is the free-expansion movement only, not a finished expansion design. Sizing the loop or joint itself, spacing pipe supports and guides, and checking the resulting stress at anchors and branch connections are separate steps that belong to a full piping stress analysis, especially on anything beyond a short, low-temperature run.
