How this is calculated
Speed ratio (no slip): N2 = N1 × (D1 / D2) — the classic inverse-diameter belt-drive relationship, independent of belt speed.
Belt (pitch line) speed: v = π·D1·N1 / 60, converted from mm/min to m/s.
Slip: whenever load torque exceeds the entered belt torque capacity, the driven pulley's actual RPM is derated below the ideal value in proportion to the overload — modelling a slipping belt losing grip, not a hard cutoff.
Open vs crossed: an open (uncrossed) belt drives both pulleys in the same rotational direction; a crossed belt reverses the driven pulley — toggle it above to see both.
The top belt strand is shown as the tight (power-transmitting) side and the bottom as the slack return side — a simplification; which side is actually tight depends on rotation direction and layout in a real installation.
