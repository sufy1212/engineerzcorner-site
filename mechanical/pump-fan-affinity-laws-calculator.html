About the Pump/Fan Affinity Laws Calculator

Given a known operating point — flow, head or pressure, and power at one speed — this projects what those values become at a new speed, using the affinity laws: flow scales linearly with speed, head/pressure scales with the square, and power scales with the cube. That's the relationship behind estimating the energy savings from slowing a VFD-driven pump or fan down, or predicting performance at a speed you haven't tested yet.

These relationships hold for speed changes on a fixed impeller or fan wheel. Trimming an impeller's diameter instead of changing its speed follows a related but distinct set of affinity relationships, and neither accounts for changes in system curve, efficiency at off-design points, or NPSH margin — those still need checking separately.
