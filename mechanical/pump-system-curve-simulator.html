How this is calculated
Pump curve (100% speed): quadratic H(Q) = H₀ − aQ − bQ² fitted through shutoff (0, H₀), BEP (Qbep, Hbep) and runout (Qmax, 0).
Speed scaling (affinity laws), for speed ratio r = speed% / 100: H(Q) = r²H₀ − arQ − bQ².
System curve: Hsys(Q) = Hstatic + KQ², with K = (friction loss at BEP flow) / Qbep².
Operating point: solved analytically where the two curves intersect, (b+K)Q² + arQ + (Hstatic − r²H₀) = 0.
Efficiency at the operating point: a parabola peaking at BEP efficiency, η(Q) = ηbep·[1 − ((Q−Qbep)/Qbep)²], floored near zero.
Hydraulic power: P = ρgQH / η (Q in m³/s), shown in kW.
Simplified quadratic pump-curve fit and parabolic efficiency model for quick sizing/teaching, not a substitute for manufacturer curve data and a full system hydraulic study (fittings, elevation profile, control valve losses).
