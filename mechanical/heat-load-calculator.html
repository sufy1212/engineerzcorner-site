How this is calculated
Airflow mode: Qs(W) = 1.2 · V(L/s) · ΔT(°C)  (using ρ·cp ≈ 1.2 kJ/m³·K for air)
Quick mode: Qs(kW) = Area(m²) · Intensity(W/m²) / 1000
1 kW = 3412.14 BTU/h = 0.284 tons of refrigeration.

About the Heat Load Calculator

This works out a first-pass sensible cooling load from a space's dimensions, occupancy, and common heat sources — walls, glazing, lighting, equipment, and people — and converts the result between kW, BTU/h, and tons of refrigeration so it's easy to compare against equipment nameplate ratings in whichever unit they're specified in.

It covers sensible heat only, so a space with significant occupancy, cooking, or process moisture needs a latent load added separately before selecting equipment. Treat the number this returns as a quick sizing estimate for early-stage decisions — a contractual cooling-load calculation still needs a full room-by-room load run through a proper psychrometric method under ASHRAE or CIBSE guidance.
