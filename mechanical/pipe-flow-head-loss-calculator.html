How this is calculated
Re = vD/ν  ·  Laminar (Re<2300): f = 64/Re  ·  Turbulent: Swamee-Jain f = 0.25 / [log₁₀(ε/3.7D + 5.74/Re⁰·⁹)]²
hf = f·(L/D)·(v²/2g)  ·  hminor = ΣK·(v²/2g)  ·  Δp = ρg(hf+hminor)
Method: Darcy-Weisbach with the Swamee-Jain explicit friction-factor approximation — standard for full-bore turbulent pipe flow, accurate to within about 1% of the implicit Colebrook-White equation over its normal range.
Transitional flow (2300<Re<4000) is unstable in reality; this sheet applies the turbulent formula through that band as a practical approximation, same as most design tools.
Practical sizing method — for critical or long transmission mains, verify against a full network model (e.g. EPANET) and manufacturer C-values / friction charts.
