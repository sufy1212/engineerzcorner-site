Tune the Tank

Set the proportional and integral gains, press run, and watch the tank fill and take a sudden draw-off. Meet the specs for overshoot, settling time and disturbance dip on four very different tanks.

Stars: 0 / 12 4 tanks
  • 1The tank starts at 20% and must reach 55%. At 60 s a sudden draw-off opens the outlet for 9 s.
  • 2You set Kp (reacts to the error) and Ki (removes the offset). Each tank has three specs, one star per spec met.
  • 3Two specs let you advance. Gains do not transfer between tanks: a tank is an integrator, so its valve gain sets what works.

About Tune the Tank

The model is a pure integrator, level change = (inlet − outlet) × valve gain, with first-order valve lag and a PI controller that stops integrating when the valve is saturated. The specs and the three scenarios are teaching targets, not a tuning method for a real loop.

The theory behind the hints is in Level Control of an Integrating Process, and you can explore the default tank in the level control simulator.
