About this converter

IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT and CEFR all measure English proficiency, but they use different scales, different test formats, and — critically — different underlying methodologies, so there's no single official conversion table that all universities and visa authorities agree on. The equivalencies shown here are drawn from publicly available concordance tables that test providers and universities commonly reference, but they're approximate, especially in the middle of each scale where score bands can overlap.

Because of that, a converted score here is a planning estimate, not a substitute for checking the specific minimum score a particular university, program, or visa category actually requires — some set requirements in IELTS-equivalent terms even when you've sat a different test, and some set separate minimums per skill (reading, writing, listening, speaking) rather than just an overall band. When in doubt, check the admissions or immigration page directly, since they periodically update which tests they accept and at what score.

Score bands are approximate concordances, not official conversions — universities and visa authorities set their own accepted score, so always confirm the exact requirement with the institution or immigration body directly.
