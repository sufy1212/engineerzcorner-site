About this tool

Most universities set a word or character limit on the statement of purpose, and that limit is usually enforced strictly — an SOP that runs over isn't just penalized on style, it's sometimes truncated automatically by the application portal itself, cutting off your closing paragraph mid-sentence. This tool gives a live word count, average sentence length, and an estimated reading time, so you can see at a glance whether a draft is within a typical target range before you paste it into an application form with a hard limit.

Sentence length and reading time are included because they're rough proxies for readability, not because there's a single correct number — an SOP made up of long, dense sentences can be technically within the word limit and still read as harder to follow than a shorter, more direct one. Admissions committees read a large volume of these in a short window, so clarity tends to matter more than sophistication of language; use the stats here as a quick check, not as the actual measure of whether the SOP is effective.

Word/sentence counting is a simple whitespace and punctuation split — it won't perfectly match every word processor's count, but it's close enough to check you're within a stated limit.
