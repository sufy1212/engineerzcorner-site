About this estimate

The monthly figures here are rough planning estimates built from typical rent, food, transport and utility costs per city, adjusted by accommodation type and a lifestyle multiplier — they're meant to give a realistic ballpark, not a precise budget. Actual costs vary a lot within any one city depending on neighbourhood, how far from campus you're willing to live, and whether housing is arranged through the university or found independently on the open market.

This figure is also worth treating as a floor rather than a ceiling: it doesn't include one-off costs like a security deposit, initial furnishing, flights, or health insurance, all of which land in the first month or two and can be substantial. Because many visa applications require proof of funds against a specific published cost-of-living figure — sometimes higher than what a student would actually spend day to day — always check the university's or immigration authority's own official minimum funds requirement rather than relying on this estimate alone for that purpose.

Cost of living is one input among many — always cross-check against a university's own published living-cost estimate for the visa application, since some countries set a required minimum funds figure.
