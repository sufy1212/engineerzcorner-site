    About this planner

    This tool works backward from a program's intake date, since most of the deadlines that actually matter during a study-abroad application don't get set by the applicant — they get set by other parties, each on their own schedule. A scholarship committee, a visa office, and a university admissions office rarely share a calendar, and the applicant is the one who has to reconcile all three into a single working timeline.

    The rough sequence used here — English test booking, transcript and recommendation requests, SOP drafting, application submission, offer, financial documentation, and visa lodgement — reflects a typical order, not a fixed rule. Some steps run in parallel rather than strictly in sequence (a scholarship application often needs to go in before an offer is even confirmed, for instance), and visa processing times in particular vary widely by country and by season. Treat the generated dates as a planning buffer, not a guarantee, and always cross-check the specific deadlines published by the university and the relevant visa authority directly.
