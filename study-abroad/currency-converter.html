About this converter

This tool pulls a live mid-market exchange rate and converts in both directions, which is useful for sanity-checking a tuition figure, a scholarship amount, or a cost-of-living estimate quoted in a currency you don't think in day to day. The mid-market rate is the reference rate banks and services use among themselves — it's the number you'll see quoted in the news, not necessarily the number you'll actually get.

In practice, banks and remittance services add a margin on top of the mid-market rate, and that margin varies a lot between providers — sometimes several percent, which adds up quickly on a tuition-sized transfer. When it's time to actually move money for a deposit, tuition payment, or living expenses, it's worth comparing the effective rate (including fees) across two or three transfer services rather than assuming your regular bank offers the best deal by default.

Live rates come from the European Central Bank's daily reference feed (via frankfurter.app) and update once a day — the rate your bank or card actually applies almost always includes a margin over this market mid-rate, so treat this as a planning estimate, not a quote for an actual transfer.
