This is a general-purpose arithmetic estimate, not a lookup of any specific country's official minimum funds rule — those rules (fixed amounts, blocked-account requirements, bank statement duration/seasoning rules, sponsor-affidavit formats) vary by destination and change periodically. Use this to sanity-check whether your own numbers add up to something reasonable, then confirm the exact required amount and acceptable proof format on the actual visa/immigration page for your destination.
A planning estimate only — official student-visa financial requirements (fixed minimums, blocked accounts, bank-statement seasoning rules) are set by each country's immigration authority and change periodically. Always confirm the exact current requirement on the official visa page before applying.
