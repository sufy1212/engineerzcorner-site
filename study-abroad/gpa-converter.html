Why this needs a converter at all

Grading scales aren't standardized internationally, and there's no single official formula that every university, WES-style credential evaluator, and immigration point system agrees on for converting between them — a percentage-marked transcript, a 10-point CGPA, and a 4.0 or 4.3 GPA all describe academic performance differently, and the same underlying result can convert to a noticeably different number depending on which approximation is used.

This converter uses commonly published approximations as a planning estimate — useful for gauging roughly where you'd land before submitting an application, or for filling in an optional GPA field on a form built for a different grading system. It is not a substitute for an official credential evaluation (WES, ECE, or a university's own admissions office conversion), which several universities and most immigration point systems specifically require rather than accepting a self-calculated conversion.

Grade-scale conversions are approximate — universities, credential evaluators (WES/ECE) and immigration point systems each use their own method, so always confirm which one a specific application actually requires.
