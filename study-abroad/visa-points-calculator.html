About this estimate

Skilled-migration and study-linked points systems (the kind used by Australia, Canada and similar programs) score applicants across a fixed set of factors — typically age, education level, work experience, and language proficiency — and require a minimum threshold before an application is even eligible to be considered further. This calculator is a simplified, illustrative version of that structure, built to show roughly how those factors interact and trade off against each other, not to reproduce any single country's actual points test.

Real points systems are considerably more detailed than what's modelled here — they often include extra points for a partner's skills, regional or state nomination, specific in-demand occupations, and other criteria that shift periodically as immigration policy changes. Treat a result from this tool as a rough directional estimate only, and always check the current, official points calculator published by the specific country's immigration authority before making any actual decisions based on eligibility.

Points thresholds move with each intake round and there's no public feed a tool could pull them from automatically, so this is a fixed, simplified model rather than live data — treat it purely as a rough, comparative estimate and confirm your real score and the current cutoff on the relevant government portal (SkillSelect, Express Entry, etc.) before relying on it.
