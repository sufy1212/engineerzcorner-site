About this calculation

This uses a standard reducing-balance EMI formula — the same method most banks use — where each monthly payment covers that month's interest on the remaining principal first, with the rest going toward paying down the principal itself. The moratorium period (typically the study duration, sometimes plus a short grace period after) is when many education loans don't require EMI payments yet, but interest usually still accrues during that window and gets added to the principal once repayment begins, which is why the moratorium interest is broken out separately here.

Actual loan terms vary meaningfully between lenders: some charge simple interest during the moratorium rather than compounding it, some offer a lower rate if a parent or guardian co-signs, and processing fees, prepayment penalties, and floating-versus-fixed rate structures all affect the real total cost beyond what a straightforward EMI formula captures. Use this tool to compare loan offers on a like-for-like basis, but confirm the exact repayment schedule and fee structure with the lender directly before signing.
