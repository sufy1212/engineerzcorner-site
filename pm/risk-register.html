Scoring Risk Consistently

Each risk is rated on how likely it is (1–3) and how severe its consequence would be (1–3); multiplying the two gives a score from 1 to 9, which is then banded into Low, Medium, or High priority. That gives a consistent, comparable way to rank a list of risks that would otherwise just be an unordered brainstorm.

This 3×3 method is deliberately simple — good enough to keep a working risk list current and prioritized on most projects. A large, complex, or safety-critical project may need a more formal quantitative risk method with a finer probability/impact scale and documented mitigation ownership.

Score = Probability (1–3) × Impact (1–3). Priority: 1–3 Low, 4–6 Medium, 7–9 High. ← PM Index
