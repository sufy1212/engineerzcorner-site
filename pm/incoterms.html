Incoterms

Incoterms 2020 — who's responsible for what, and where risk transfers
PM Index PM.01 RACI Matrix PM.02 Risk Register PM.03 Task Tracker PM.04 Meeting Minutes PM.05 EVM PM.06 Team Directory PM.07 Commercial Terms PM.08 Incoterms PM.09 PMP / PMBOK PM.10 Punch List PM.11 Permit to Work PM.12 Glossary
Incoterms 2020 (published by the International Chamber of Commerce) define who arranges and pays for carriage, insurance and customs, and exactly where risk of loss or damage passes from seller to buyer — they don't cover who holds title to the goods, or payment terms, which are separate matters set elsewhere in the sale contract. Summarized here in plain language for quick reference; for a live contract, work from the actual ICC Incoterms 2020 rulebook.
Any Mode of Transport Sea & Inland Waterway Only

Suitable for road, rail, air, sea or multimodal shipments — the most flexible group, and the recommended default for container/multimodal freight.

Reading the Table

A few things that trip people up.
PointWhy it matters
Risk vs cost aren't the same lineIn CPT/CIP/CFR/CIF, the seller pays freight (and sometimes insurance) to the destination, but risk still passes much earlier — at origin. Cost and risk transfer at different points on purpose.
"C" terms are shipment contractsCPT, CIP, CFR and CIF are shipment (not arrival) contracts — the seller's obligation is met when goods are handed to the carrier, regardless of what happens after.
Incoterms don't set payment termsWhen and how the buyer pays (letter of credit, net 30, advance payment) is a separate commercial agreement, not something the Incoterm itself defines.
Incoterms don't transfer titleOwnership of the goods is a matter of the sale contract/local law, not the Incoterm — a shipment can have risk pass to the buyer while title stays with the seller until paid, or vice versa.
Always name the placeEvery Incoterm needs a named place (e.g. "FOB Shanghai", "DAP Rotterdam") to actually mean anything — the three-letter code alone doesn't specify where risk transfers.
General reference only — always confirm current ICC Incoterms 2020 wording (and specify the named place) directly in the sale contract. ← PM Index
