Tracking Actions to Closure

A lightweight way to capture what needs doing, who owns it, and by when, without the overhead of a full project-management platform — each row is one action item with an owner and a due date.

The overdue flag compares each item's due date against today and its current status, automatically flagging anything past due that isn't marked Done. It doesn't account for weekends or holidays in that comparison. Since nothing here is saved between visits, treat it as a working scratchpad for a meeting or shift handover, and export or copy the list before closing the tab.

It's built for the situations where spinning up a task in a full PM platform is overkill — a short punch list from a site walk, action items out of a quick huddle, or a handover between shifts — where a simple owner-and-due-date table gets the job done faster than opening a heavier tool.
