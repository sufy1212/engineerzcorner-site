How this is calculated
Cost = Attendees × Rate × (Minutes / 60)
Annual Cost = Cost × occurrences/year (52 for weekly, 12 for monthly, 1 for one-time)
A blunt but useful gut-check for standing meetings — if the annual number looks large relative to the value the meeting delivers, that's usually a sign to shorten it, cut the attendee list, or drop the cadence.
