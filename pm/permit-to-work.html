A Consistent Permit Record

A structured checklist for documenting isolation points, safety checks, and sign-offs before work begins on hazardous equipment or systems, then generating a clean, copyable record of what was checked and who signed off.

This is a drafting and record-keeping aid, not a substitute for your site's official permit-to-work and lockout-tagout (LOTO) procedure. Always follow your organization's actual documented process and obtain every required physical sign-off and verification in person before work begins.
