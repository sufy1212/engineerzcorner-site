How this is calculated
Contingency = Base × Contingency%  ·  Cost Baseline = Base + Contingency
Management Reserve = Cost Baseline × MR%  ·  Total Budget = Cost Baseline + Management Reserve
Contingency covers identified risks within the project's control (the PM can spend it without extra approval on most projects). Management reserve covers unforeseen scope and typically needs sponsor/management sign-off to release — that's why it's layered on top of the baseline rather than the raw base estimate.
A budgeting structure, not a substitute for a proper quantitative risk assessment on large or complex projects. ← PM Index
