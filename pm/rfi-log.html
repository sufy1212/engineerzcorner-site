Keeping an RFI Log Useful

A Request for Information (RFI) is a formal question raised to the designer, engineer, or owner when the contract documents are unclear, conflicting, or missing information needed to proceed with the work. An RFI log tracks every request from submission to answer, so nothing gets lost in email threads and every open question has a visible due date.

The overdue flag here is simple — it just compares the response due date you enter against today's date for any RFI still marked "Open." As with the other PM tools on this site, nothing is saved between visits — generate and copy the summary, or export it, before closing the tab.
