From Notes to a Clean Summary

A structured way to capture meeting details, attendees, discussion points, and action items during or right after a meeting, then generate a consistently formatted summary you can copy straight into an email or shared document.

Keeping the same structure meeting after meeting — same fields, same order — makes it far easier for anyone to scan past minutes later and quickly find what was decided and who owns what, compared to free-form notes that vary in format every time.

Nothing typed here is saved between visits, so treat it as a working scratchpad for the meeting itself: fill it in live or right after while details are fresh, then copy the generated summary out to wherever the record actually needs to live — email, a project wiki, or a shared drive.
