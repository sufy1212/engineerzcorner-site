Closing Out Snags Systematically

A punch list (or snag list) tracks the individual items that need fixing before a space, system, or project can be signed off — each entry captures what's wrong, where it is, which trade owns it, and how urgent it is.

The open-item count and priority breakdown update automatically as items are marked resolved, giving a quick read on how close a handover is without manually recounting. As with the other PM tools on this site, nothing is saved between visits — copy or export the list before closing the tab.
