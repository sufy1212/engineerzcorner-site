Why Only Approved COs Move the Baseline

A change order shifts the contract's cost or schedule baseline once it's formally approved — until then it's a pending claim or proposal, not a committed change. Keeping pending and rejected items visible (but excluded from the revised totals) avoids the common trap of quietly treating a submitted CO as if it were already agreed, which can hide real budget and schedule exposure until it's too late to react.
