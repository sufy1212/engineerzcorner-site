A Quick Reference Contact Sheet

A single place to list everyone on a project — name, role or trade, company, and contact details — useful for a project kickoff, a site induction pack, or a quick reference handed to someone new joining the team.

This tool doesn't sync or save anything between sessions, so it's meant as a working scratchpad you fill in and export, not a permanent system of record. Keep the authoritative version of your team's contact information wherever your organization already manages it.

Where it earns its keep is the moment a directory needs to exist quickly and doesn't yet — day one of a job site, a new subcontractor joining mid-project, or a printed sheet for an induction pack. Fill it in, export, and hand it over; there's no account or sync step slowing that down.
