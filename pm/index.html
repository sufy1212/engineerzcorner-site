Project Management — Index

Fig. P1 — Tasks against the schedule line
Twelve standalone tools for running the team and commercial side of a project, not the engineering side — who's doing what, what could go wrong, what's overdue, what got said in the last meeting, how the budget and schedule are actually tracking, the contract vocabulary that ties it together, and the PMBOK structure it all maps back to. Pick one below.
PM.01
RACI Matrix Builder
Set your team, list your tasks, and mark who's Responsible, Accountable, Consulted or Informed for each one.
PM.02
Risk Register
Log risks with probability & impact — the score and priority (low/medium/high) update automatically.
PM.03
Action / Task Tracker
Task, owner, due date, status — overdue open items are flagged automatically against today's date.
PM.04
Meeting Minutes & Action Items
Attendees, discussion notes and action items in one form — generate a clean, copyable minutes summary.
PM.05
Earned Value Management (EVM)
PV, EV, AC and BAC in — CPI, SPI, cost/schedule variance, EAC, ETC and VAC out, with plain-English status.
PM.06
Team Directory
Name, role, email and phone for everyone on the project — one place to look instead of five inboxes.
PM.07
Commercial Terms of Project
Plain-language reference for contract, payment and claims vocabulary — LOI, retention, VOs, EOT, LDs, DLP and more.
PM.08
Incoterms
Incoterms 2020 reference — where risk transfers, who arranges carriage, and who pays freight and insurance.
PM.09
PMP / PMBOK Reference
Process groups, knowledge areas, schedule/risk vocabulary and PMP credential basics, all in one reference page.
PM.10
Punch List / Snag Tracker
Log defects with location, trade and priority — open/critical counts update live, and it generates a copyable plain-text summary.
PM.11
Permit to Work / LOTO Checklist
Isolation points, safety checks and sign-off fields — generates a copyable permit record for your site file.
PM.12
PM Glossary
A–Z definitions for the contract, schedule and site vocabulary used across engineering projects.
