Reading a Loading Sheet

Utilization = allocated hours ÷ available hours for the period, usually a week. Over 100% means more work is assigned than there's time to do it — a leading indicator of missed dates or burnout before it shows up in status reports. Under roughly 70% often means either genuine spare capacity or work that isn't being tracked at the task level; either way it's worth a look.

This is a snapshot for one period — a real resource plan usually looks at this week-by-week across the project, since allocation shifts as tasks start and finish.
