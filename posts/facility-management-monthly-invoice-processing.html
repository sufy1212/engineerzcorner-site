Facility Management: Monthly Invoice Processing

Every FM expense sorts into three categories — utility, service, consumables — and every invoice moves through the same ten-step process from receipt to filing. Here's the framework, illustrated with a worked tracker.

Why categorize FM invoices

Facility management runs on a steady stream of monthly invoices — utility bills, vendor service charges, consumable top-ups. Sorting every expense into one of three categories before it hits the approval chain keeps verification consistent, gives finance a predictable audit trail, and makes it obvious who signs off on what. It's the difference between chasing paperwork and running a process.

Good ProcessA repeatable path for every invoice, regardless of category.
Better ControlClear ownership at each verification stage.
Smooth OperationsVendors get paid on time; services don't get interrupted.
Happy WorkplaceUtilities, housekeeping and safety supplies never run short.

Monthly invoice process flow

Regardless of category, every invoice moves through the same ten checkpoints before it's filed:

  1. 1
    Invoice receivedFrom the vendor, against the billing period.
  2. 2
    Document verificationInvoice matches vendor details, period and format.
  3. 3
    PO verificationLine items and rates match the purchase order.
  4. 4
    GRN / SESGoods Receipt Note or Service Entry Sheet confirms delivery.
  5. 5
    Qty / service verificationQuantities or service levels checked against GRN/SES.
  6. 6
    GST & tax checkTax rates and computation validated.
  7. 7
    FM / manager approvalSign-off from the facility manager.
  8. 8
    Finance submissionApproved invoice routed to finance.
  9. 9
    PaymentReleased per agreed vendor terms.
  10. 10
    Invoice filingArchived for audit & compliance.

Key takeaways

  • ✓Always verify PO, GRN/SES and the invoice itself before approval — skipping any one breaks the audit trail.
  • ✓Check GST correctness and keep supporting documentation attached to every invoice, not just the summary.
  • ✓Follow the agreed SLA for invoice processing and payment — vendors on utility and AMC contracts depend on it.
  • ✓Keep all invoices organized by category for audit & compliance — a tracker makes gaps visible before they become disputes.
