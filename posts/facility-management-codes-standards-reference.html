Facility & Property Management: Key Codes & Standards Reference

The codes and standards that give FM professionals a defensible framework for safety, compliance, asset reliability and sustainability — organized the way IFMA scopes the job, from maintenance through building codes.

Why codes matter for FM

Codes and standards provide the framework for safe, compliant, efficient and sustainable facility and property management. They help FM professionals manage assets, people and services throughout the facility lifecycle while ensuring risk mitigation, regulatory compliance and operational excellence.

Ensure Safety & ComplianceMeet legal, regulatory and industry requirements.
Optimize PerformanceImprove asset reliability, energy efficiency and occupant comfort.
Manage RiskReduce operational, financial and safety risks.
Support SustainabilityDrive resource efficiency and environmental stewardship.
Deliver ValueEnhance service quality, lifecycle value and stakeholder satisfaction.

Key takeaways

  • ✓No single code covers facility management — ISO, ASHRAE, NFPA, OSHA, NEC/IEC and local building codes each own a different slice.
  • ✓Local codes (e.g. Saudi SBC) and international codes (IBC, IFC, IMC) are typically applied together, not as substitutes for one another.
  • ✓Where codes overlap or conflict, the contract, the local regulator, or the more conservative requirement usually governs.
  • ✓Documentation — O&M manuals, asset registers, as-built drawings — is what turns compliance into something you can actually demonstrate at handover or audit.
