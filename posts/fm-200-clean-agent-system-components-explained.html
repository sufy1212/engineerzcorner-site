FM-200 (Heptafluoropropane) is a clean agent fire suppression system widely used to protect critical assets and sensitive environments. Instead of dousing a space in water or foam, it works by rapidly absorbing heat and interrupting the chemical reaction of fire, extinguishing it within seconds — without leaving any residue or causing damage to the equipment it's protecting. That's the whole reason it exists: some rooms simply can't afford a suppression agent that ruins what it just saved.

Key benefits

  • ✓Clean agent — no residue, no mess left behind after discharge.
  • ✓Safe for people — low toxicity at design concentration.
  • ✓Fast acting — extinguishes fire within seconds of release.
  • ✓Non-conductive — ideal for electrical and electronic equipment.
  • ✓Environmentally responsible — zero ozone depletion potential.

Applications

Typical protected spaces
  • Data centers & server rooms
  • Control rooms & electrical panels
  • Telecommunication facilities
  • Museums, archives & libraries
  • Hospitals & laboratories
Why FM-200 fits
  • No wet residue on sensitive gear
  • Non-conductive around live equipment
  • Safe at design concentration for occupied rooms
  • Compact cylinder footprint
  • Rapid knockdown limits downtime

FM-200 system diagram: components & function

A complete FM-200 installation is a closed loop from detection to discharge — each component below exists to move the signal one step closer to releasing the agent, or to let a person override the sequence manually.

1
FM-200 Cylinder
Stores the FM-200 agent as a liquid under pressure, ready for discharge into the piping network.
2
Solenoid Valve
Opens on command from the control panel to release FM-200 into the piping system.
3
Nozzles
Distribute the agent evenly across the protected area once it reaches the discharge header.
4
Control Panel
Monitors the detection loop continuously and activates release once a fire condition is confirmed.
5
Fire Detection System
Detects the fire — smoke or heat — and sends a signal to the control panel.
6
Manual Release Station
Lets a person trigger the system directly, bypassing automatic detection in an emergency.
7
Pressure Switch
Monitors cylinder pressure after discharge and sends a status signal back to the control panel.
Automatic sequenceDetection system confirms a fire → signal reaches the control panel → panel commands the solenoid valve open → FM-200 releases through the piping and nozzles → pressure switch confirms discharge back to the panel.
Manual overrideThe manual release station sits in parallel with automatic detection — pulling it opens the solenoid valve directly, so the system can still be triggered if detection is slow or a person spots the fire first.
Did you know?FM-200 is stored as a liquid and discharged as a gas, which means it requires less storage space compared to other gaseous suppression systems — a real factor when cylinder room space is limited.

Key takeaways

  • ✓FM-200 extinguishes by absorbing heat and interrupting the fire's chemical chain reaction — not by displacing oxygen the way CO2 does.
  • ✓Seven components carry the sequence: detection system, control panel, manual release station, solenoid valve, cylinder, pressure switch and nozzles.
  • ✓The manual release station runs in parallel with automatic detection, so a person can trigger discharge without waiting on the detection loop.
  • ✓Being stored as a liquid and discharged as a gas keeps the cylinder footprint smaller than other gaseous agents needing the same protected volume.
  • ✓Design and installation should follow NFPA 2001 and the applicable local fire code, verified against the manufacturer's data sheet.
