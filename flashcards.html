About This Flashcard Set

48 symbols spanning Electrical, P&ID/Automation, HVAC/Ductwork, Civil/Structural, Solar, and General Drafting — click a card to flip it and reveal the symbol's name and meaning, then use the discipline filter to drill one category at a time instead of the full mixed set.

The order shuffles on demand and nothing is saved between visits, so it works as a quick repeatable drill rather than a tracked course — useful for building fast visual recognition of common drawing symbols before reading a real P&ID, single-line diagram, or structural plan.

Full Symbol List

All 48 symbols in this drill, for reference or a quick scan without flipping through the deck one at a time.

SymbolDisciplineMeaning
ResistorElectricalA rectangle (IEC) represents a fixed resistor that opposes current flow, dissipating energy as heat.
CapacitorElectricalTwo parallel lines, one often curved, representing two conductive plates that store energy in an electric field.
InductorElectricalA series of loops or humps, representing a coil that stores energy in a magnetic field and opposes changes in current.
Circuit BreakerElectricalA switch symbol with a break in the line, representing a protective device that can be manually or automatically opened.
Earth / GroundElectricalA set of horizontal lines decreasing in width, representing a connection to the general mass of earth.
TransformerElectricalTwo coils separated by parallel lines (the core), representing a device that transfers energy between windings via a magnetic field.
Three-Phase MotorElectricalA circle containing the letter M, sometimes with '3~' beside it, representing a rotating electrical machine.
FuseElectricalA rectangle with a line through it, representing a sacrificial overcurrent device that melts to break the circuit.
Push Button (NO)ElectricalTwo contact lines with a gap and an arrow/plunger above, representing a normally-open momentary switch.
Lamp / IndicatorElectricalA circle with an X or cross inside, representing an indicator lamp or light source in a control circuit.
BatteryElectricalAlternating long and short parallel lines, representing a DC voltage source (cells in series).
Gate ValveP&ID / AutomationTwo triangles meeting at their points, representing a valve used mainly for full-open or full-close isolation.
Control ValveP&ID / AutomationA valve symbol with an actuator box on top, representing a valve that modulates flow based on a control signal.
Check ValveP&ID / AutomationA circle with a triangle inside pointing in the flow direction, allowing flow one way only.
Centrifugal PumpP&ID / AutomationA circle with a triangle inside pointing toward the discharge, representing a rotodynamic pump.
Pressure Transmitter (PT)P&ID / AutomationA circle labeled PT, representing an instrument that measures pressure and transmits a signal to the control system.
Flow Transmitter (FT)P&ID / AutomationA circle labeled FT, representing an instrument that measures flow rate and transmits a signal to the control system.
Level Transmitter (LT)P&ID / AutomationA circle labeled LT, representing an instrument that measures the level of liquid or solids in a vessel.
Temperature Transmitter (TT)P&ID / AutomationA circle labeled TT, representing an instrument that measures temperature and transmits a signal to the control system.
PLC / Logic BlockP&ID / AutomationA rectangle with an internal function label, representing a programmable logic controller or a discrete logic function.
Orifice Plate (Flow Restriction)P&ID / AutomationA plate with a hole shown in a pipe run, used to create a pressure drop for differential-pressure flow measurement.
Solenoid ValveP&ID / AutomationA valve symbol with a coil/box actuator above it, representing an electrically-actuated on/off valve.
Supply DuctHVAC / DuctworkA rectangle with a filled triangle arrowhead, representing a duct carrying conditioned air to a space.
Return DuctHVAC / DuctworkA rectangle with a hatched/cross-hatched arrowhead, representing a duct returning air back to the air handler.
Fire DamperHVAC / DuctworkA rectangle in a duct run with a diagonal line and 'FD' marking, representing a damper that closes automatically in a fire.
Diffuser (4-way)HVAC / DuctworkA square with an X or four internal triangles, representing an outlet that spreads supply air in multiple directions.
ThermostatHVAC / DuctworkA circle with a T inside, representing a temperature-sensing control device mounted on a wall.
Air Handling Unit (AHU)HVAC / DuctworkA labeled rectangle box, representing the assembly that filters, heats, cools and moves air through a duct system.
Pin SupportCivil / StructuralA triangle beneath a point, representing a support that resists translation in both directions but allows rotation.
Roller SupportCivil / StructuralA triangle on circles, representing a support that resists translation perpendicular to the rolling surface only.
Fixed SupportCivil / StructuralHatching along a solid base, representing a support that resists translation in all directions and rotation.
Column SectionCivil / StructuralA filled or hatched square/rectangle in plan view, representing a vertical load-bearing structural member.
Section Cut LineCivil / StructuralA thick line with arrowheads and a label, indicating where a section view has been cut through a drawing.
North ArrowCivil / StructuralAn arrow, often inside a circle, indicating true or project north for orienting the drawing.
CenterlineGeneral DraftingAn alternating long-dash, short-dash line, marking the axis of symmetry of a part or feature.
Dimension LineGeneral DraftingA line with arrowheads at both ends and a number above it, indicating a measured distance between two points.
Weld Symbol (Fillet)General DraftingA right-triangle flag on a reference line, representing a fillet weld joining two members at an angle.
Revision CloudGeneral DraftingA scalloped, cloud-shaped outline drawn around a drawing area, flagging a recent change from the previous issue.
PV Module / PanelSolarA rectangle divided into a grid, representing a single photovoltaic module — the basic building block of a solar array.
String InverterSolarA box with a stylized waveform inside, representing the device that converts a module string's DC output into grid-compatible AC.
MPPT Charge ControllerSolarA box with an angled tracking-curve icon, representing a controller that tracks a solar array's maximum power point while charging a battery bank.
Combiner BoxSolarAn enclosure with several parallel lines converging to one output, representing the point where multiple module strings are joined into a single combined feed.
MC4 ConnectorSolarTwo interlocking circular connector halves, representing the standard weatherproof, locking DC connector used between module and string cables in the field.
DC Disconnect SwitchSolarA break in the line with an angled blade, representing a manually operated switch that isolates DC power between the array and the inverter.
Battery BankSolarA rectangular cell body with a small terminal cap, representing the battery bank that stores DC energy from the array for later use.
Bypass DiodeSolarA triangle-and-bar diode symbol, representing the device that routes current around a shaded or faulted cell group inside a module's junction box.
Rapid Shutdown DeviceSolarA power-button style icon at module level, representing the module-level device that drops array conductors to a safe low voltage on command.
Bi-Directional Utility MeterSolarA meter face with a two-directional arrow, representing the meter that measures energy flowing both from and, under net metering, back to the grid.
