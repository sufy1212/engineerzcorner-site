Process/Instrumentation — Calculator Index

Fig. P1 — Vessel with relief valve, level and control valve on the outlet
Eight standalone process/instrumentation calculators — relief valve sizing, vessel volume & level, process line sizing, control valve cavitation/flashing, dilution purge gas sizing, heat exchanger LMTD sizing, pipe wall thickness (ASME B31.3), and an NPSH available calculator — plus a learning-notes reference page, a process equipment symbol library, step-by-step guides, and head-to-head comparisons. Like Automation and Mechanical, these aren't chained together, so each one is self-contained.
PR.01
Relief Valve (PSV) Sizing
Required orifice area for a pressure relief valve on liquid or vapor service, per the simplified API 520 sizing equations, with nearest standard orifice letter.
PR.02
Vessel/Tank Volume & Level
Total and partial-fill liquid volume for a horizontal or vertical cylindrical tank from diameter, length/height and liquid level.
PR.03
Process Line Sizing (Erosional Velocity)
Actual pipe velocity vs the API RP 14E erosional velocity limit, from mass flow, fluid density, pipe ID and service type.
PR.04
Control Valve Cavitation/Flashing Check
Service sigma (σ) index from upstream/downstream pressure and vapor pressure, flagging flashing, cavitating or clean flow.
PR.05
Dilution Purge Gas Sizing
Purge gas volume and time to dilute an enclosure or vessel from an initial to a target concentration, for a given purge flow rate.
PR.10
Heat Exchanger Sizing (LMTD)
Log-mean temperature difference and required heat transfer area, for counter-current or co-current flow arrangements.
PR.11
Pipe Wall Thickness (ASME B31.3)
Pressure design minimum wall thickness, plus corrosion allowance and mill-tolerance check against a candidate nominal schedule.
PR.12
NPSH Available Calculator
NPSH available derived from vessel pressure, vapor pressure, elevation and suction-line losses, checked against NPSH required for a cavitation margin.
PR.13
Valve Type Selector
Function, fluid and priority in — a recommended valve type (gate, globe, ball, butterfly, check, plug, needle, diaphragm or relief) and comparison table out.
PR.06
Learning Notes
Reference-only theory notes on relief systems, vessels, process piping and valve phenomena — not a calculator.
PR.07
Symbol Library
Click-to-explore reference of process equipment P&ID symbols — vessels, pumps, relief devices, exchangers and process valves.
PR.08
Engineering Guides
Step-by-step how-to guides — sizing a relief valve, checking for cavitation, dilution purging and reading a process P&ID.
PR.09
Comparisons
Head-to-head comparisons — relief valve vs rupture disk, cavitation vs flashing, PSV vs PRV terminology, and more.
