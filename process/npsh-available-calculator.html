Reading the Margin

A margin under about 0.5 m is a real cavitation risk and usually means changing the layout — raising the source vessel, shortening or up-sizing the suction line, reducing fittings, or lowering the pumping temperature to cut vapor pressure. A margin of roughly 0.5–1 m is workable but tight, especially if operating conditions vary; 1 m or more gives a comfortable buffer against normal variation in level, temperature and wear.

This is a first-pass calculation for a single suction line at steady flow — it doesn't cover vortexing at low tank level, entrained gas, or NPSH margin requirements some pump standards (like API 610) add on top of the manufacturer's bare NPSHr curve.
