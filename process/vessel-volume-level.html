How this is calculated
—
Vertical: V = πR²h. Horizontal: partial volume uses the circular-segment area A = R²(θ−sinθ)/2 with θ = 2·acos((R−h)/R), then V = A·L. 1 m³ = 264.172 US gal = 6.2898 bbl (42-US-gal oil barrel).
Flat-head approximation — for dished, elliptical or spherical heads, add the head volume separately. ← Process Index
