Picking a valve type from scratch usually comes down to three questions: what job does it need to do, what's flowing through it, and what matters most — tight shutoff, precise throttling, low pressure drop, a compact footprint, or cost. Answer them below for a starting recommendation, then check it against the comparison table. For sizing a valve you've already selected, see Automation's Valve Sizing calculator; for how each type works mechanically, see the Valve Types & Parts blog post.
