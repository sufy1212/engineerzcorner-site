Models continuous dilution purging of a well-mixed enclosure or vessel — displacing an initial O₂ or contaminant concentration down to a target level with a steady purge gas flow. Assumes perfect, instantaneous mixing (no dead zones or short-circuiting), which is optimistic for real vessels — apply a safety margin on top of the calculated purge time or volume for a confined-space or vessel-entry purge.

How this is calculated
—
Perfectly mixed dilution model: C(t)/C₀ = exp(−Qt/V), so N = ln(C₀/C₁) turnovers are needed, purge time t = (V/Q)·N, and total purge gas consumed = Q·t = V·N.
Ideal-mixing model — real vessels typically need extra time/volume margin for incomplete mixing and dead zones. ← Process Index
