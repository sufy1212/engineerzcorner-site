Why the Mill Tolerance Matters

The pressure design thickness from the B31.3 equation is the true minimum needed to hold pressure — but pipe as manufactured is allowed to run thinner than its nominal (listed) wall by the mill's tolerance, commonly 12.5% for seamless pipe under API 5L/ASTM A106/A53. That means a nominal wall must be inflated before ordering: dividing the pressure-plus-corrosion requirement by (1 − tolerance) gives the nominal wall that guarantees the pipe is thick enough even in a worst-case thin spot straight off the mill.

This tool checks the t < D/6 case, which covers the overwhelming majority of process piping. For thick-wall pipe where t approaches or exceeds D/6, or for any pressure design under external pressure (vacuum/collapse), B31.3 requires a different equation entirely — that's outside this preliminary check.
