Motor FLC / Starting Current

Full-load current from motor rating, and starting current by starting method
Full-load current (FLC) from a motor's rated power, voltage, efficiency and power factor, then the starting current that follows from it under a chosen starting method. Use FLC as the design current feeding cable sizing and breaker rating; use starting current to check upstream voltage dip and breaker short-time withstand.

How this is calculated
Three-phase: I_FL = P×1000 / (√3 × V × cosφ × η)  ·  Single-phase: I_FL = P×1000 / (V × cosφ × η)
Starting current: I_start = I_FL × multiple
Doesn't check the resulting voltage dip on the upstream supply during starting (see SH.06 Tx/Genset Sizing for that check), or the thermal withstand of the starter itself. Star-delta's stated multiple is its line current during the star phase; the transition to delta briefly draws a second, separate inrush.
Full-load and starting current only — locked-rotor withstand, thermal overload settings and starter sizing need the motor manufacturer's data sheet.
