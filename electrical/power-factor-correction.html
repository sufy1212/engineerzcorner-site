About Power Factor Correction Sizing

Enter the load's real power and its existing power factor, along with the power factor you're targeting, and the calculator works out the reactive power a correction capacitor bank needs to supply to close that gap. It's the same relationship utilities use to set power factor penalty thresholds on a bill, so this is often the starting point for justifying a correction project.

The result is the kVAR needed, not a finished bank design — actual capacitor bank sizing needs to account for how the bank is staged and switched, and any site with significant harmonic-producing loads (VFDs, UPS systems) should be checked for resonance before adding fixed capacitance. Those checks fall under a full power quality review rather than this sizing step.
