Panel GA Maker

Front elevation — sections, modules and busbar chamber for one switchboard or DB enclosure
Electrical Index Panel GA Maker
Set the overall enclosure size, add vertical sections (incomer, bus coupler, outgoing feeders), then stack modules — breakers, contactors, blanks — inside each section from top to bottom. The busbar chamber runs full width along the top or bottom. The elevation redraws to scale automatically and flags any section whose modules don't fit its height.
