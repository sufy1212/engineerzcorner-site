Panel Schedule Builder

Circuit-by-circuit panel schedule with automatic per-phase totals, imbalance check and panel loading
Build out a panel schedule the way it actually gets used on a job — one row per circuit, breaker size, which phase(s) it lands on, and its connected load. As you add circuits, this totals the connected load on each phase, flags how far out of balance they are, and checks the total against the panel's main breaker rating. Nothing is saved between visits — generate and copy the schedule before closing the tab.

How this is calculated
Per-phase VA = Σ (circuit load ÷ number of legs it spans)  ·  Imbalance % = (max phase − min phase) / average phase × 100  ·  Total current ≈ total VA / (√3 × VLL) for 3-phase, or per-leg VA / 120 for split-phase
This is a connected-load schedule, not a demand-load calculation — it does not apply NEC demand factors for continuous loads, motor loads, or diversity, and the 80% continuous-load derating on the main breaker isn't applied automatically. A panel loaded near 100% of the main rating on paper may still need a demand-factor check before it's actually acceptable. Phase imbalance above roughly 5-10% is generally worth rebalancing circuits to reduce neutral current and uneven voltage drop.
