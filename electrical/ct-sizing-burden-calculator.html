CT Sizing & Burden Calculator

Ratio · burden (VA) · accuracy limit factor · short-circuit withstand
Selects a current transformer for a protection or metering circuit: the standard primary ratio, the connected burden including lead-wire loss, the standard rated burden to specify, and a saturation check against your maximum fault current. Lead loss is the classic surprise — it scales with the square of the secondary current, so a 5 A secondary carries 25× the lead burden of a 1 A secondary over the same cable. How this is calculated
Primary rating Ipn = next standard value ≥ Iload × (1 + headroom)  ·  Rlead = ρ × L × k / A, with ρ = 0.0175 × (1 + 0.00393 × (T − 20)) Ω·mm²/m  ·  Sb = Sdevice + Isn² × (Rlead + Rcontact)  ·  Sct = Isn² × Rct  ·  Effective ALF = ALF × (Sn + Sct) / (Sb + Sct)  ·  n = If / Ipn  ·  Saturation voltage = n × Isn × (Rct + Sb/Isn²), multiplied by (1 + X/R) for full DC offset  ·  Ith = If × √t  ·  Idyn ≈ 2.5 × If (2.6 at 60 Hz)
A P-class CT keeps its rated composite error up to the point where the effective ALF is at least the overcurrent multiple n. If the effective ALF falls short, the CT can saturate during the fault and the relay sees a distorted, reduced current. Fix it with a larger cross-section, a shorter run, a 1 A secondary, a higher ratio, a higher rated ALF or rated burden, or a PX / TPS class CT. The DC-offset figure matters mainly for high-speed and differential schemes; P-class CTs are not specified for transient offset.

Reference: CT Terms

TermMeaning
Rated primary / secondaryThe CT ratio, e.g. 500/5 A. Standard IEC primaries: 10, 12.5, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75 and their decimal multiples
Burden (VA)Apparent power the secondary circuit draws at rated secondary current — devices plus leads plus contacts
Rated burden SnThe burden the CT is guaranteed to meet its class at. IEC standard values: 2.5, 5, 10, 15, 30 VA
5P20Protection class: composite error ≤ 5% at 20 × rated primary current, at rated burden
Accuracy limit factor (ALF)The multiple of rated current up to which the class error is guaranteed at rated burden. It drops as the connected burden rises
Class X / PXDefined by knee-point voltage and Rct instead of a fixed ALF; used for differential and high-impedance schemes
Ith / IdynRated short-time thermal current (usually 1 s) and rated dynamic (peak) current the CT must survive
First-pass CT selection using the IEC 61869-2 effective-ALF relationship and the IEEE C37.110 saturation-voltage approach. Confirm against the CT manufacturer's data (excitation curve, Rct at working temperature, ratio list), the relay manufacturer's stated CT requirements, and your protection-coordination study.
