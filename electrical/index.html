Electrical — Drawing Index

Eight linked worksheets for LV distribution studies — pick a sheet below. Numbers you calculate on one sheet carry through to the next, the way they would on a real drawing set.

SH.01 Short Circuit — SH.02 Voltage Drop — SH.03 Selectivity — SH.04 Cable Sizing — SH.05 Panel Estimate — SH.06 Tx / Genset — SH.07 Busbar / Enclosure —
SH.01
Short Circuit
Fault current from source to last DB, per IEC 60909-0. Builds a distribution tree and sums impedance down each path.
SH.02
Voltage Drop
Cumulative voltage drop through a feeder chain, checked against IEC 60364-5-52 limits.
SH.03
Selectivity / TCC
Time-current grading between breakers, with a plotted TCC chart and pairwise coordination margins.
SH.04
Cable Sizing
Ampacity and thermal withstand checks per IEC 60364-5-52 / 5-54, against your own reference tables.
SH.05
Panel Estimate
Incomer and outgoings roll up into a BOQ, busbar sizing, enclosure dimensions and a budgetary cost total.
SH.06
Tx / Genset Sizing
Transformer kVA from connected load and demand factor; standby generator kVA from essential load and a motor-starting voltage-dip check.
SH.07
Busbar / Enclosure
Busbar CSA sizing with short-circuit thermal and electrodynamic withstand checks; enclosure dimensioning with a natural-convection heat dissipation check.
SH.08
Earthing Rod Resistance
Single vertical rod-to-earth resistance from soil resistivity, rod length and diameter.
SH.09
Power Factor Correction
Capacitor kVAR needed to move a load from its existing power factor to a target power factor.
SH.14
Lighting / Illumination
Luminaire count for a target lux level, using the lumen method.
SH.15
Conduit Fill
Cable cross-section vs. conduit internal area, checked against a max fill %.
SH.16
Motor FLC / Starting Current
Full-load current from motor rating, plus starting current for DOL, star-delta, soft-start and VFD methods.
SH.17
Arc Flash Incident Energy
Simplified theoretical (Ralph Lee) incident energy, arc flash boundary and approximate PPE category, for first-pass screening.
SH.18
Harmonics / THD Estimate
Current THD and K-factor from individual harmonic magnitudes, checked against a generic IEEE 519 reference limit.
SH.10
Engineering Guides
Twelve step-by-step how-to guides — cable sizing, MCCB selection, IEC 61439, LV panel design and more.
SH.11
Comparisons
Ten head-to-head equipment comparisons — MCB vs MCCB vs ACB, TN vs TT vs IT, PVC vs XLPE cable, motor starting methods and more.
SH.12
Symbol Library
Click-to-explore reference of common electrical schematic and single-line symbols.
SH.13
SLD Creator
Build a single line diagram by adding elements and picking what each feeds from — auto-drawn, exportable as SVG, printable.
SH.19
Panel GA Maker
Front-elevation drawing of one switchboard/DB — sections, stacked modules, busbar chamber and cable entry, with an auto-generated module schedule.
SH.20
Battery / UPS Autonomy Sizing
Required battery Ah and string configuration from load, backup time, DOD, aging and temperature factors.
SH.21
Cable Schedule Builder
Add cables with from/to, size, length and type — auto-totals, exportable as CSV or a project file.
SH.22
Earthing Grid Resistance
Ground grid resistance (Sverak/IEEE 80 equation) from soil resistivity, grid area, buried conductor length and burial depth.
SH.23
Three-Phase Power & Current
Convert between kW, kVA, kVAR, current and power factor for single- or three-phase loads.
SH.24
Energy Cost / kWh Bill
Estimate monthly and annual electricity cost from load power, runtime, and a flat or tiered rate.
