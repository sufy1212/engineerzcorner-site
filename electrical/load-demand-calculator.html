Load / Demand Calculator

Connected load to maximum demand, by load category
Not every connected watt runs at once — a demand factor accounts for that within a single load schedule, and a diversity factor accounts for it again when several feeders are summed at a common bus upstream. Add each load category with its connected load and demand factor to get individual demand loads, then apply an overall diversity factor to size the incoming supply or main breaker.

How this is calculated
Per category: Demand = Connected × DF. Totals: ΣDemand, then Max Demand = ΣDemand × Diversity. Current (3φ): I = MaxDemand×1000 / (√3 × V × PF). Current (1φ): I = MaxDemand×1000 / (V × PF).
Demand factors are typical planning values (lighting ~0.9, general power/sockets ~0.4–0.7, HVAC ~0.8–1.0, motors ~0.75–0.85 depending on duty) — always confirm against your governing code or utility's demand-factor tables, since these vary by occupancy type and jurisdiction. This sizes the supply/main breaker only; still run the Cable Sizing and Breaker/Fuse Sizing calculators downstream for the actual conductor and protective device selection.
