Motor-starting voltage dip check — your largest single motor (or largest group starting together):
How the starting check works
Rule-of-thumb generator sizing used for a first-pass estimate, not a substitute for the manufacturer's transient stability software (Cummins PowerSuite, Cat SpecSizer, etc.), which models saturation and AVR/governor response the way this simplified formula can't:

Motor rated kVA = motor kW ÷ rated power factor
Motor starting kVA = motor rated kVA × starting multiplier (apparent power scales with current, at constant voltage)
Required generator kVA (dip check) = Motor starting kVA × Xd' ÷ Allowable dip

DOL starting is typically 5–7× rated current; star-delta roughly 2–3×; soft-starter or VFD 1.2–2×. Xd' of 20–30% is typical for a standard synchronous alternator — ask your generator supplier for the actual value on large or sensitive starting loads.
