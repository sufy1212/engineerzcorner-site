How this is calculated
Long-time zone (Ir ≤ I < Isd): t = tr·(Ir/I)² — a common simplified I²t=k long-time curve.
Short-time zone (Isd ≤ I < Ii): t = tsd — flat definite time (assumes I²t ramp is off, the common default).
Instantaneous zone (I ≥ Ii): trips in <40 ms, treated as t≈0 for margin purposes.
Margin = t(upstream breaker at this fault current) − t(this breaker at this fault current). Selective if margin ≥ your minimum, AND the fault current stays below the upstream breaker's own Ii — if it doesn't, the upstream breaker also sees it as instantaneous and both trip together regardless of time settings.
Simplified two-curve check at one operating point — doesn't model I²t ramps in the ST zone or manufacturer tolerance bands. For contractual coordination studies, verify against the actual 3VA/3WA datasheet curves in SIMARIS or the manufacturer's selectivity tables.
