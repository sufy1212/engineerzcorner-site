Cable cross-section vs. conduit internal area
Checks what percentage of a conduit's internal cross-sectional area is filled by the cables you're pulling through it, against a maximum fill limit. Keeping fill below the limit leaves room to actually pull cables through without damaging insulation, and lets trapped heat escape.

How this is calculated
Conduit area: Ac = π/4 × d²  ·  Cable area: Acable = π/4 × od² × n  ·  Fill: Acable / Ac × 100
Assumes all cables are the same outer diameter — for mixed cable sizes, sum each cable's individual area separately. Fill limits are a mechanical-pull and heat-dissipation guideline, not a substitute for a full derating/ampacity check on the cables themselves.
