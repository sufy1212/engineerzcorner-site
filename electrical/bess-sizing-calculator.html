BESS Sizing Calculator

Usable energy · nameplate capacity · power rating · C-rate
Sizes a commercial/grid-scale Battery Energy Storage System from a required load and duration — separate from the residential off-grid autonomy sizing in the UPS Autonomy tool and the Solar Sizing Calculator's daily-load battery step. The distinguishing feature here is the power-vs-energy check: a BESS sized purely for kWh can still fail to deliver the kW you need if its C-rate is too low — a common real-world sizing miss.

How this is calculated
Usable Energy = Power × Duration × (1+Margin)  ·  Nameplate = Usable Energy / (DoD × Inverter Eff × Battery Eff)  ·  Power Rating = Power × (1+Margin)  ·  Max Power at C-rate = Nameplate × C-rate
If Max Power at the chosen C-rate is below the required Power Rating, the system is power-constrained — you need a higher C-rate battery or more nameplate capacity than energy alone would suggest, purely to deliver the required kW. This is a genuinely common miss in first-pass BESS sizing: teams size to kWh and only discover the power shortfall after quoting.

Reference: BESS Terms

TermMeaning
Nameplate capacityTotal rated energy storage, before DoD/efficiency losses (kWh)
Usable energyNameplate × DoD — what's actually available to discharge
Power ratingMaximum continuous power the system can deliver (kW)
C-rateDischarge/charge speed relative to capacity — 1C empties a full battery in 1 hour
Round-trip efficiencyEnergy out ÷ energy in over a full charge/discharge cycle
Sizing relationships (usable energy, DoD, C-rate, round-trip efficiency) follow standard, publicly documented BESS engineering definitions — not a proprietary model.
