Earthing Grid Resistance

Ground grid resistance to remote earth — Sverak / IEEE 80 equation
Estimates a buried earthing grid's resistance to remote earth using Sverak's simplified equation from IEEE Std 80 — for a substation or plant earth mat rather than a single rod (see SH.08 for that). Soil resistivity varies with moisture and season, so treat this as a design-stage estimate and confirm the installed grid with a fall-of-potential test.

How this is calculated
R = ρ × [ 1/L_T + 1/√(20A) × ( 1 + 1 / (1 + h√(20/A)) ) ]  (Sverak's equation, IEEE Std 80)
Assumes a roughly uniform soil resistivity and a reasonably even conductor grid — layered soils, unusually shaped grids, or grids dominated by a few deep rods will need the full IEEE 80 method or software rather than this single-value estimate. Doesn't check touch/step voltage, which is the other half of a proper earthing design.
Grid resistance to remote earth only — touch and step voltage, conductor sizing for fault current, and soil resistivity testing are separate checks a full earthing design needs. ← Electrical Index
