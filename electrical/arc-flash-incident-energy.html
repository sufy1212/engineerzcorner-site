Arc Flash Incident Energy

Screening estimate — not a substitute for a full IEEE 1584-2018 study
Two public-domain empirical methods, both referenced in NFPA 70E Informative Annex D: the Doughty-Neal-Floyd equations for systems ≤600V (validated on 16–50 kA test data, box or open-air), and the Ralph Lee equation for open-air arcs above 600V. Neither is the proprietary IEEE 1584-2018 regression model — this is a first-pass screening tool only. Do not use this output to select PPE or set arc-flash labels; commission a proper IEEE 1584-2018 study by a qualified person for that.

Doughty-Neal-Floyd equations: R.L. Doughty, T.E. Neal, H.L. Floyd, "Predicting Incident Energy to Better Manage the Electric Arc Hazard on 600 V Power Distribution Systems," IEEE Trans. Industry Applications, 2000 — the empirical basis carried into IEEE 1584-2002 and referenced in NFPA 70E Informative Annex D. Ralph Lee open-air model: R.H. Lee, "The Other Electrical Hazard: Electric Arc Blast Burns," IEEE Trans. Industry Applications, 1982, as adapted in NFPA 70E Annex D.6.
