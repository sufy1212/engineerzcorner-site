Arc Flash Incident Energy

Simplified theoretical estimate — screening only, not a substitute for a full study
Uses the Ralph Lee theoretical arc-flash equation to give a rough, conservative incident energy estimate at a stated working distance, plus the distance at which incident energy falls to the 1.2 cal/cm² arc-flash-boundary threshold. This is a simplified screening tool only — it ignores equipment class, electrode configuration and gap, enclosure type and grounding, all of which the empirical IEEE 1584-2018 model accounts for. Do not use this output to select PPE or set arc-flash labels; commission a proper IEEE 1584-2018 study for that.

How this is calculated
E = 2.142×10⁶ × V × Ibf × t / D²  (cal/cm², Ralph Lee equation)  ·  D_boundary = √(2.142×10⁶ × V × Ibf × t / 1.2)
PPE category bands shown (Cat 1 ≈4, Cat 2 ≈8, Cat 3 ≈25, Cat 4 ≈40 cal/cm² minimum arc rating) are a common informal reference only, not the NFPA 70E table itself. Always confirm against a current arc-flash study and your governing standard's actual PPE category table before specifying PPE or labels.
