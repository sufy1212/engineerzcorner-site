Battery / UPS Autonomy Sizing

Required battery capacity and string configuration for a given backup time
Estimates the battery Ah capacity needed to carry a UPS or DC load for a stated backup time, working back from the load through inverter efficiency, depth of discharge, an aging allowance, a temperature correction and a design margin. This is a simplified constant-power method — a full IEEE 485 sizing exercise uses the battery manufacturer's actual discharge-rate tables rather than a flat Ah figure, and should be used to confirm the final selection.

Assumes a constant power draw over the backup period rather than the battery's actual voltage-sag discharge curve, so treat the Ah figure as a first-pass sizing number.
