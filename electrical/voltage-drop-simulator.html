How this is calculated
Load current: I = P / (√3·V·PF) (3-phase) or I = P / (V·PF) (1-phase).
Voltage drop: Vd = k·I·L·(R·cosφ + X·sinφ)/1000, where k = √3 for 3-phase or k = 2 for 1-phase, L in metres, R/X in Ω/km.
%Vd = Vd / V × 100. End voltage plotted linearly along the run since drop is proportional to distance at constant current.
R values are typical 70°C copper conductor resistances per IEC 60228-style tables; aluminium uses RAl ≈ 1.64 × RCu.
