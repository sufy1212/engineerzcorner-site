How this is calculated
THD = √(Σ Ih%²)  ·  I_rms = I₁×√(1+Σ Ih_pu²)  ·  K = Σ(Ih_pu²×h²) / Σ(Ih_pu²)  (including fundamental, h=1)
K-factor is what you'd hand to a transformer supplier to select a K-rated transformer able to handle this harmonic loading without exceeding its winding temperature rise. The 5% reference shown is IEEE 519's commonly-cited general limit for lower-voltage systems — the actual applicable limit in the standard depends on the Isc/IL ratio at your point of common coupling and needs a proper power-quality study to confirm.
Distortion estimate from stated harmonic magnitudes only — a real installation needs a power-quality survey to measure actual harmonic content.
