How this is calculated
Daily energy = P × hrs/day  ·  Monthly energy = Daily energy × days/month
Flat: Cost = Monthly energy × rate
Tiered: Cost = (kWh up to Tier 1 limit × Tier 1 rate) + (remaining kWh × Tier 2 rate)
Annual cost = Monthly cost × 12 (assumes a repeating month — swap in seasonal figures for a more precise annual estimate).
Doesn't include fixed monthly charges, demand charges, taxes, or time-of-use peak/off-peak splits — add those separately from your actual bill.
Estimate only — check your utility bill for fixed charges, taxes, and demand charges not modeled here.
