Earthing Rod Resistance

Single vertical rod electrode — simplified Dwight formula
Resistance to earth of a single vertical driven rod, from soil resistivity, rod length and diameter. Doesn't cover multiple-rod arrays, mutual coupling between rods, or seasonal soil resistivity variation — measure with a real earth tester after installation.

Soil & Rod

Varies hugely by soil type — wet clay can be under 50 Ω·m, dry sand or rock can exceed 1000 Ω·m. Use a measured value where possible.

Common target values (set by your governing code, not by this tool) are often in the 1–25Ω range depending on the installation — a single rod frequently isn't enough on its own and is combined into a rod array or grid.
How this is calculated
R = (ρ / 2πL) · [ln(4L/d) − 1]
The simplified Dwight formula for a single vertical rod driven flush with grade. Real-world resistance also depends on soil moisture, temperature and compaction, and is normally confirmed with a fall-of-potential earth resistance test after installation.
Single-rod estimate only — multi-rod arrays, ground grids and soil variation need a full earthing system design.
