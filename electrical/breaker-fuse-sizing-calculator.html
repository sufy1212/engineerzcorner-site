How this is calculated
Rating selection: smallest standard In from the series 6, 10, 13, 16, 20, 25, 32, 40, 50, 63, 80, 100, 125, 160, 200, 250, 315, 400, 500, 630 A satisfying In ≥ Ib and In ≤ Iz. Cable protection: I2 = k2 × In, checked against I2 ≤ 1.45 × Iz.
k2 = 1.45 for circuit breakers to IEC 60898 and 1.6 for gG fuses to IEC 60269 — both already satisfy I2 ≤ 1.45×Iz automatically once In ≤ Iz for breakers; fuses need the explicit check since k2 is higher. Instantaneous magnetic trip bands (typical): Curve B 3–5×In, Curve C 5–10×In, Curve D 10–20×In — check this against inrush/starting current (e.g. motor starting) to avoid nuisance tripping. This selects a standard device rating only — always verify discrimination/selectivity with upstream and downstream devices, and confirm the breaking capacity (Icu/Icn) against the prospective fault current from the Short Circuit Calculator.
