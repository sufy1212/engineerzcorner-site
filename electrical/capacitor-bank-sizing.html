About Capacitor Bank Sizing

Once you know the reactive power a correction bank needs to supply, the next question is a physical one: how much capacitance, at what current, and how should it be split into switching steps. This calculator converts the kVAR figure into a per-phase capacitance in microfarads, works out the current the bank draws at rated voltage, and divides the total into equal steps so an automatic power-factor controller can switch capacitance in and out to track a varying load.

Connection type matters more than it looks: a delta-connected bank needs only a third of the capacitance a star-connected bank would need for the same kVAR at the same line voltage, because each capacitor in a delta bank is wired across the full line-to-line voltage rather than the lower phase voltage. That's the main reason most low-voltage industrial banks are built delta-connected — less capacitance per kVAR of correction.
