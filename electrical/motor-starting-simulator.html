How this is calculated
Torque-speed curve: a smooth spline through (0%, LRT), (25%, pull-up), (80%, breakdown), (rated speed, 100%FLT), (100%, 0) — the standard datasheet presentation, so it needs no motor equivalent-circuit data.
Current-speed curve: a smooth spline from locked-rotor current down to FLC, staying high until near rated speed (typical induction-motor shape).
Star-delta: torque and current both scale by 1/3 below the transition speed (line voltage reduced to 1/√3 in star), then jump to the full DOL curve above it.
VFD: current held flat at the set current limit up to rated speed; available torque is approximated as proportional to current at constant flux (T% ≈ I×FLC%) — a first-order approximation valid in the constant-torque (below base speed) region.
Load torque — constant: flat at the entered %FLT. Variable (fan/pump): scales with (speed/100)².
Stall check: flags star-delta if the load torque exceeds the star-phase motor torque anywhere below the transition speed — the motor can't accelerate past that point in star.
Simplified single-operating-point model for comparison and teaching — no thermal, inertia, or transient-spike calculation. For contractual studies, use manufacturer torque-speed data and a full time-domain start-up study.
