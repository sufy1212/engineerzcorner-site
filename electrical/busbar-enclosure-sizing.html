Busbar Current Rating

Sizes the copper section from rated current and current density, then rounds up to a standard flat-bar size.
1.2 A/mm² is a common conservative rule of thumb for air-insulated copper busbar in an enclosure — verify against IEC 61439 temperature-rise data (or your switchgear supplier's tables) for anything final. Higher density (1.4–1.6) is sometimes used for well-ventilated, lightly loaded busbar.

Short-Circuit Withstand

Thermal (I²t) check per IEC 60865, plus an electrodynamic force check on the support brackets.
Ik'' and Ip come straight off the Short Circuit tab for this board (Ip is shown there too — same idea as κ√2·Ik''). k is the IEC 60865 material constant for a 1s adiabatic temperature-rise check (short-time withstand, from a starting temp of ~30°C to a limit of ~180°C for PVC-touching busbar — standard textbook value, not project-specific).
MINIMUM CSA FOR THERMAL WITHSTAND 0.00 mm²
ELECTRODYNAMIC FORCE PER SUPPORT 0.00 N
F = 2×10⁻⁷ × Ip² / d × L (IEC 60865 simplified rigid-conductor formula). This checks the support bracket only — it doesn't check bending stress in the bar itself against copper's yield strength, which matters for long spans or thin bars. For a contractual short-circuit withstand certificate, use type-tested busbar systems or a full IEC 60865 calculation.
