Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and how ads and analytics work here.
Last updated: . This page explains what information this site collects and how it's used.

What this site is

This site is a free reference toolkit of engineering calculators (electrical, mechanical, HVAC, civil, automation, solar and general-purpose conversion tools), plus project management reference pages, an interview prep section, a blog, browser games (Quickfire, Daily Quiz, Symbol Flashcards, Fault Finder, Defect Finder, Machine Fixer, Signal Chain), and a Study Abroad section (score conversion, cost of living, loan EMI, visa points, university fees, currency conversion, an SOP word checker, and an application timeline planner). All calculations and games run in your browser — the numbers you type or paste into a sheet, including any text pasted into the SOP word checker, are not sent to a server and are not stored by us.

Information we don't collect

This site does not require an account, login, or profile to use anything on it. We don't collect names, addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or government ID numbers, and we don't ask for them anywhere. Calculator inputs, PM worksheet entries, game progress, and any text you paste into a tool (including the SOP word checker) stay in your browser's memory for that session and are never transmitted to or stored on a server we control.

Newsletter / email signup

Some pages on this site include an optional email signup form, provided through a third-party email service provider. If you choose to submit your email address, it is sent directly to that provider to add you to a mailing list, and is used only to send you updates from this site. Submitting the form is entirely optional — you can use every calculator, guide and game on this site without providing an email address. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in any email you receive, or by contacting us directly (see below).

Server logs and hosting

Like virtually every website, the hosting infrastructure that serves these pages automatically records basic technical request information (such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp) as part of normal web server operation. This is standard hosting-level logging, not something this site actively collects or analyzes for individual identification, and it's used only for things like security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing outages.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for basic functionality — for example, remembering a dark-mode preference, in-progress PM worksheet data you've entered, or your position in a multi-step tool — and for analytics and advertising, described below. Functional cookies/local storage generally stay on your device and aren't transmitted anywhere; analytics and advertising cookies are set by the respective third-party providers.

Third-party vendors and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's past visits to this site or other websites. Third-party advertising vendors' use of cookies enables them to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. This site does not control what these third parties do with data collected through their own cookies — their use is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Analytics

This site may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are visited, roughly how often, what device or browser type is common, and similar high-level usage trends. This data is used in aggregate to improve the site and is not used to identify individual visitors personally.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for engineers, students, and other adult or near-adult professional/technical audiences, and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site (for example, via the newsletter form), contact us and it will be removed.

Data retention

Since calculator, worksheet, and game inputs are never sent to or stored on a server by this site, there's nothing for us to retain or delete on that front — closing or refreshing the tab clears it. For the newsletter, your email address is retained by the third-party provider until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be removed. Aggregate analytics data is retained by the analytics provider according to their own retention settings, not individually tied back to you.

Your choices and rights

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely. You can opt out of personalized advertising from participating vendors through the Digital Advertising Alliance or Google Ads Settings. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email you receive. Depending on where you're located, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, or deletion requests) under laws like the GDPR or a regional equivalent — for the newsletter list, these requests are best directed to the email provider via the unsubscribe/preferences link, or to us directly and we'll pass them along.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and no personal data beyond an optional email address (handled entirely by the third-party newsletter provider) passes through it. Because there's no account system or server-side data store for visitor inputs, there's no user database on this site to be exposed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site's tools, vendors, or practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request related to your data (such as a newsletter removal), can be sent via the Contact page.
