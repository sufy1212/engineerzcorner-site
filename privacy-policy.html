Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and how ads and analytics work here.
Last updated: . This page explains what information this site collects and how it's used.

What this site is

This site is a free reference toolkit of engineering calculators (electrical, mechanical, HVAC, civil, automation, solar and general-purpose conversion tools), plus project management reference pages, an interview prep section, a blog, browser games (Quickfire, Daily Quiz, Symbol Flashcards, Fault Finder, Defect Finder, Machine Fixer, Signal Chain), and a Study Abroad section (score conversion, cost of living, loan EMI, visa points, university fees, currency conversion, an SOP word checker, and an application timeline planner). All calculations and games run in your browser — the numbers you type or paste into a sheet, including any text pasted into the SOP word checker, are not sent to a server and are not stored by us.

Newsletter / email signup

Some pages on this site include an optional email signup form, provided through a third-party service (Brevo). If you choose to submit your email address, it is sent directly to that provider to add you to a mailing list, and is used only to send you updates from this site. Submitting the form is entirely optional — you can use every calculator, guide and game on this site without providing an email address. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in any email you receive.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality (such as remembering a dark-mode preference) and for analytics.

Third-party vendors

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's past visits to this site or other websites. Third-party advertising vendors' use of cookies enables them to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Analytics

This site may use analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns (which pages are visited, roughly how often). This data is not used to identify individual visitors personally.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of personalized advertising from participating vendors through the Digital Advertising Alliance or Google Ads Settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact details provided on the About page.
