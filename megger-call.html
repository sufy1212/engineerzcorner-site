Rule card
  • LV minimums: SELV/PELV 250 V DC, 0.5 MΩ. Up to 500 V: 500 V DC, 1 MΩ. Above 500 V: 1000 V DC, 1 MΩ. A 230 V circuit tested at 250 V still needs 1 MΩ.
  • Temperature correction: R40 = R × 0.5(40−T)/10
  • Motor 1-minute minimums at 40 °C: random-wound 5 MΩ, modern form-wound 100 MΩ, older or field winding rated kV + 1 MΩ.
  • PI = R10 / R1. Minimum 2.0 for class B, F, H; 1.5 for class A. Both checks must pass.

About Megger Call

Every sheet is a real kind of insulation resistance result: an LV circuit tested to IEC 60364-6 / BS 7671 limits, or a motor or generator winding judged on a 1-minute reading corrected to 40 °C plus the polarization index from IEEE 43. The pass or fail verdict is calculated from the rule card, so the explanation after each answer shows the working.

The full explanation is in Insulation Resistance Testing Explained. The game is practice, not a substitute for the standard edition and manufacturer limits that apply to your equipment.
