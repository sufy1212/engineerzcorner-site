About Panel Doctor

Each round hands you a real-world symptom — output stays energized after the stop command, say — plus a small diagram of that control loop. One component in it is drawn as failed: welded contacts, an open coil, a drifted timer, a seized VFD fan, or a shorted output. Diagnosing the right component from the symptom is the entire challenge.

The 26 scenarios cover PLC I/O, motor control, safety circuits, drives, and field devices rather than one repeated panel, so the same complaint can trace back to a different failed component depending on the equipment shown. It's meant to sharpen fault-to-symptom intuition, not replace field diagnostics.
