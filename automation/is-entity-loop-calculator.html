The entity concept (IEC 60079-11 / ANSI-ISA 60079-11) lets you mix certified barriers/isolators and field devices from different manufacturers without a system-level test, as long as the nameplate parameters clear four checks: the barrier's maximum fault voltage and current must not exceed what the field device can accept, and the barrier's allowable external capacitance and inductance must cover the field device's own values plus whatever the cable adds. How this is calculated

This checks the standard IEC 60079-11 entity-concept relationships only. It does not replace a full system loop certification, does not evaluate FISCO/FNICO fieldbus rules (which use different, model-specific limits), and does not account for multiple field devices sharing one barrier, multi-core/multi-pair cable capacitance coupling between cores, or the hazardous-area gas group and temperature class the parameters were certified for — all of which must also be verified against the equipment's certificates before a loop is installed.
Entity-parameter screening check only — final intrinsic safety loop approval requires the certified apparatus documentation (control drawings, certificates) for the specific gas group and temperature class.
