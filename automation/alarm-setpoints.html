How this is calculated
Trip (EU) = range_lo + %FS/100 × (range_hi − range_lo)
High-type reset = trip − deadband  ·  Low-type reset = trip + deadband
ISA-18.2 alarm management practice: HH should sit further from normal than H (and LL further than L), with enough deadband that the alarm doesn't re-trigger on process noise right at the trip point.
Positions and deadband entered here are a starting configuration — validate against your process's actual noise band and site alarm-philosophy document before commissioning.
