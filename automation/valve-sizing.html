How this is calculated
Kv = Q(m³/h) · √(SG/ΔP(bar))  ·  Cv ≈ 1.156 · Kv
This is the standard incompressible turbulent-flow formula — it gives the flow coefficient the valve needs, not a specific valve model or trim. Cross-check against the manufacturer's Cv/Kv table and confirm the valve isn't choked or cavitating at this ΔP.
Sizing coefficient only — final valve selection also needs trim characteristic, rangeability and cavitation/noise checks.
