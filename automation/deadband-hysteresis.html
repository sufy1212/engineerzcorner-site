Why deadband matters
Upper = SP + width/2  ·  Lower = SP − width/2  ·  %FS = (value − range_lo) / (range_hi − range_lo) × 100
Too narrow a deadband causes a control output or alarm to chatter (rapid on/off cycling) right at the setpoint; too wide lets the process wander further before correcting. Typical starting point: 0.5–2% of span for most control loops, wider for noisy signals.
A deadband is not the same as PID gains — it's a switching guard band, commonly used on on/off control, alarms, and digital outputs.
