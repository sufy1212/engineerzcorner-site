Learning Notes

Theory reference notes, organized by discipline, including Solar. Each section works through the fundamentals first, then builds toward the standards and equipment-specific detail that actually comes up on the job — not a dump of trivia, but the concepts the calculators and guides on this site assume you already know.

Pick a discipline below to browse its full outline, or jump straight to a section if you already know what you're after — the notes are written to be skimmed for a quick refresher as easily as read start to finish. If you'd rather test what you remember than reread it, the daily quiz pulls questions from across all of these.
